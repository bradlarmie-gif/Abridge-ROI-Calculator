Build a premium, multi-page PDF export for the Outpatient ROI Model. This is the flagship export — it represents the first impression of Abridge for many prospects and needs to be exceptional. The document should feel like a McKinsey deliverable meets Stripe's design sensibility meets Apple's confidence.

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

1. **Educational, not salesy** — Teach them how ambient AI creates value, don't pitch
2. **Premium density** — Every page should feel valuable, no wasted space
3. **Transparent math** — Every number traceable, executives can verify with finance
4. **Personalized** — Only shows their selected drivers, their numbers, their scenario
5. **Legally defensible** — "Could" not "will", estimates not guarantees, assumptions visible
