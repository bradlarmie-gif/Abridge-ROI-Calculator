# Gap Analysis / Cost Page — Final Spec

## The Job of This Page

This page answers ONE question: "What is standing still costing my organization?"

It does NOT tell them what to do. That's the summary page. This page creates the weight. The summary page gives the relief.

### Card 2: WHAT EACH LEVEL IS WORTH

For each domain, show their current level and what the NEXT level would unlock. Not the dream state. Just one step forward.

**Key tone principles:**
- Strategic partner voice, not sales voice
- Never tell them what to do operationally ("see more patients," "present to your CMO")
- Show what becomes visible or quantifiable at the next level
- Let them connect the dots on what actions to take
- Dollar ranges where math supports it, qualitative framing where it doesn't

### Card 3: 3-YEAR PROJECTION

Same graph as current implementation. Two lines.

**Updated labels:**
- Dashed gray: **"Current trajectory"**
- Solid Abridge red: **"With strategic action"**

```
3-YEAR PROJECTION
Cumulative value: current trajectory vs. strategic action
```

**Calculation:**
```
currentAnnual = sum of measured dollar values across domains
strategicAnnual = sum of next-level projections (using low-end
                  of ranges shown in Card 2)

For each time point:
  currentCumulative = currentAnnual × (months / 12)
  strategicCumulative = strategicAnnual × (months / 12)
```

Use LOW END of all ranges for the strategic line. This keeps it conservative and defensible. The gap will still be significant.

**Below the graph:**
```
Over 36 months, the gap between your current trajectory
and strategic action is approximately $[gap].

Projections based on your inputs and next-level benchmark
ranges (low end). Actual results depend on organizational
execution. Individual results vary.
```

## What This Page Does NOT Have

- No 90-day roadmap (that's the summary page)
- No "present to your CMO" directives
- No operational instructions ("see more patients")
- No export or share functionality (that's the summary page)
- No aggressive language about what they "must" do

## What This Page DOES Have

- Their deployment reality vs. benchmark (the mirror)
- What the next level unlocks for each domain (the possibility)
- The 3-year projection showing divergence (the cost of waiting)
- The sidebar making the monthly cost visceral (the urgency)

The tone throughout is a strategic partner showing you your own data and helping you understand what it means. Not telling you what to do. Not selling. Just showing.
