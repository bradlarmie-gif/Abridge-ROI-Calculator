Key Design Decisions:
1. Quartile Visualization (Not Just Averages)
Shows them where they fall relative to the distribution — more impactful than just "average."
2. Gap-Focused Framing
Every page emphasizes what they're missing, not what they have.
3. Time as Currency
Translate everything to hours/week, FTE equivalents — tangible.
4. Cost of Waiting
Show that delay = permanent value loss. Creates urgency.
5. 3-Year Compound View
Small gaps compound into massive losses over time.
6. Same Metrics as Expand
Utilization, Time Savings, Level of Service — consistent framework.
