Three fixes. Be precise.
1. 🔴 CRITICAL: Investment in the Financial Summary is being reduced by utilization/ramp — it should NOT be.
The Annual Investment section correctly shows Y1: $1,250,000 and Y2: $2,250,000 (total $3,500,000). But the 3-Year Financial Summary table shows Y1 investment as ~$613K — roughly half. This means utilization or the provider ramp is being applied to the cost side.
The rule is simple:

VALUE side = reduced by utilization, ramp, and onset delays (this is correct)
COST side = the full contracted amount, period. No ramp, no utilization reduction.
For Per Encounter pricing: Annual Investment = contracted encounters × price per encounter. Not actual encounters used × price. The customer is contracted for 500,000 encounters at $2.50 — they pay $1,250,000 regardless of how many they actually use.
For Per Unit/Mo pricing: Annual Investment = licensed providers × price × 12. Not actively documenting providers × price × 12.
For Annual License: Annual Investment = the annual fee. No adjustment.
Fix every place where investment is calculated — the monthly cash flows, the proforma table, and the payback/IRR calculations must all use the FULL contracted investment, not a utilization-adjusted number.
This is why payback shows 1 month — the investment is artificially halved, so cumulative value exceeds cumulative investment almost immediately. Once investment is correct ($1.25M in Y1), payback will move to a realistic month.

The IRR calculation has been consistently broken across multiple attempts. Rather than show a wrong number or "N/A," just remove it. Remove the IRR metric card, remove IRR from the methodology section, remove IRR from scenario comparisons. Clean removal — don't leave empty space or "N/A" placeholders.
The remaining metrics (Simple ROI, Value-to-Cost, Payback, 3-Year Net Value) are sufficient and more intuitive for the audience.

When the user selects "2-Year" contract, the Y3 $/Encounter field (and Y3 providers, Y3 price fields for all pricing models) should not be visible. Only show fields for the years in the selected contract length. Same logic: 3-Year shows Y1-Y3, Custom shows however many years are configured.
