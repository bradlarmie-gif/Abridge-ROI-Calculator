Completely rebuild the Outpatient ROI Analysis PDF export from scratch. This document is the first impression of Abridge for many prospects — it needs to be the most premium, educational, and visually stunning PDF in healthcare technology.

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

This is NOT a report template with numbers plugged in. This is a custom-crafted analysis that teaches the reader how to think about ambient AI ROI while showing them their specific opportunity.

Reference points for quality:
- Stripe's documentation and reports (clean, confident, technical but accessible)
- McKinsey deliverables (structured, rigorous, but not stuffy)
- Apple's product pages (typography, white space, visual hierarchy)
- But warmer than all of these — this is healthcare, these are people

The reader should think: "This company clearly knows what they're doing. I trust this analysis. I could send this to my CFO."

## BRAND ELEMENTS

**Colors:**
- Abridge Coral: #E85A4F (use sparingly — accents, key CTAs, brand moments)
- Value Green: #059669 (money, positive outcomes, the "good" numbers)
- Deep Gray: #111827 (primary text — confident, not wishy-washy gray)
- Medium Gray: #6B7280 (secondary text, labels)
- Light Gray: #9CA3AF (tertiary text, subtle labels)
- Background: #FFFFFF (primary), #F9FAFB (cards/sections), #F3F4F6 (subtle depth)
- Subtle Green Tint: #ECFDF5 (positive callout backgrounds)
- Subtle Red Tint: #FEF2F2 (for Abridge-branded callout backgrounds)

**Typography:**
- Use clear hierarchy: 24pt+ for hero numbers, 14pt for section titles, 11pt for body, 9pt for labels
- Numbers should be prominent — they're the stars
- Generous line height (1.5-1.6 for body text)
- Letter-spacing on section headers (subtle, 0.5-1pt)

**Layout Principles:**
- 60pt page margins minimum
- Strong left alignment for most content
- Asymmetric layouts where appropriate (not everything centered)
- Cards with subtle shadows or borders to create depth
- Intentional white space — let important things breathe
- Dense sections balanced with breathing room
