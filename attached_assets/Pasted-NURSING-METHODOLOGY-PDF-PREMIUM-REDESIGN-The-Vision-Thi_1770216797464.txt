NURSING METHODOLOGY PDF — PREMIUM REDESIGN
The Vision
This isn't a reference document. It's a teaching tool that positions Abridge as the intellectual leader on nursing value. Someone should be able to read this and walk away understanding:

Why nursing value is different
How we think about each mechanism
What we can measure vs. what we enable
How to validate it themselves
