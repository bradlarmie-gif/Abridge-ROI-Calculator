Ambient Assessment: Nursing Edition
What This Is
A pre-ROI strategic conversation. Before anyone talks about time savings, before anyone opens a calculator, before anyone sees a number — this assessment helps nursing leadership understand where documentation burden is creating cost and risk in their organization, and which value pathways would matter most if that burden were reduced.
The output isn't a dollar figure. It's clarity. The nursing executive walks away knowing: "Our biggest exposure from documentation burden is in overtime and retention. That's where we should focus the ROI conversation." Or: "Actually, our biggest issue is bedside time and quality documentation — the financial case is secondary to the clinical case."
That clarity is what makes the ROI conversation productive when it happens. And it positions Abridge as the partner who helped them think about it before anyone asked them to buy anything.

The Core Idea
Nursing documentation burden is real but it shows up differently at every organization. Some hospitals are drowning in overtime from end-of-shift charting. Some are hemorrhaging money on travel nurses because they can't retain staff. Some are focused on getting nurses back to the bedside. Some are worried about documentation quality and survey readiness.
The mistake most vendors make is jumping straight to "we'll save you 20 minutes per shift" and then trying to turn that into a dollar figure. The nursing executive's reaction is: "Okay but 20 minutes of what? And where does that value actually land for us?"
The Ambient Assessment: Nursing Edition answers that question FIRST. It says: "Before we talk about time savings, let's understand your documentation burden landscape. Where is it costing you the most? What would matter most if it were reduced?"
Then, when you're ready for the ROI conversation, you're not starting from scratch. You're starting from: "We know our biggest pathways are OT and retention. Show me what the numbers look like for those."

Pathway 4: Time at the Bedside
TIME AT THE BEDSIDE
When nurses spend less time documenting, that time
can go to direct patient care. This is hard to put
a dollar value on — but it's often the most important
pathway to nursing leadership.

HOW THIS SHOWS UP
Nurses spending more time at workstations than at
bedsides. Patient experience scores that could be
higher. Care quality metrics that depend on
observation and presence.

○ Yes — it's something we talk about
○ Yes — it's a key initiative with metrics

○ Informally

WHAT THIS PATHWAY MEANS

to patient experience, safety, and quality outcomes
is well-established — though it requires more than
documentation changes alone.

DOCUMENTATION QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE

HOW THIS SHOWS UP
