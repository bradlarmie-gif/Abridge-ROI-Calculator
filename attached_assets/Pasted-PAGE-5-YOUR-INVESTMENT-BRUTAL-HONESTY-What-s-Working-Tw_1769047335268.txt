PAGE 5: YOUR INVESTMENT - BRUTAL HONESTY
🎯 What's Working:

✅ Two pricing models - Per provider vs Enterprise gives flexibility
✅ Contract term selector - Clean buttons, clear choice
✅ The 2.7x ROI callout - Big, dark card, creates confidence
✅ "Your Configuration" summary - Shows the math transparently
✅ Implementation fee toggle - Optional cost, good UX

🚨 What's NOT Working:

"Your Investment" is weak framing - Sounds like we're asking for money. This should be "Value vs. Investment" or "Your Return"
The layout feels transactional - Left side config, right side ROI feels like we're hiding the punchline
ROI calculation is buried - The 2.7x should be ABOVE the fold, not mid-page
Annual Investment number ($90K) - Feels big and scary without immediate context
Missing: Comparison to current state - What are they spending NOW on documentation burden, scribes, overtime, etc.?
No confidence builders - Where's the "risk-free pilot" messaging? Money-back guarantees? Success stories?


MY REDESIGN PHILOSOPHY
This isn't an "investment page" - it's a COMPARISON page.
The mental model should be:

Left side: What you're spending today (hidden costs, current state)
Right side: What you'll spend with Abridge (transparent, better outcome)

Key Reframe:
"You're not buying software. You're trading $90K/year for $245K/year in realized value."
