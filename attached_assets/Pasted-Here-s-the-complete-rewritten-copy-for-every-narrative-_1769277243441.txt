Clinician Retention
The Theory
CopyTurnover is expensive. Replacing a physician costs $400K-$800K+ when you factor in recruiting, lost revenue during vacancy, onboarding, and the 12-18 months it takes a new hire to reach full productivity.

Documentation burden is consistently cited as a top driver of burnout. It's not the only driver—but it's one of the few that's directly addressable without restructuring compensation or patient panels.

We model this carefully. Not all turnover is preventable. Not all preventable turnover is burnout-related. And not all burnout is documentation-driven. We apply what we call the "Rule of Thirds": documentation contributes to roughly half of burnout, Abridge reduces documentation burden by about 70%, yielding ~35% theoretical impact. We round down to 30% to stay conservative.
