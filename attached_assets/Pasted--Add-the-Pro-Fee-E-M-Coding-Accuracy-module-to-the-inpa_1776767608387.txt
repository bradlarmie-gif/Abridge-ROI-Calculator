  Context (read before implementing)                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                         
  This module models the revenue impact of coding accuracy — ensuring physician charges reflect the clinical complexity that was actually present and documented. The mechanism: ambient capture puts the full 
  clinical conversation into the note, making E/M level selection based on Medical Decision Making (MDM) more accurate. This is right-coding. Never use the word "upcoding" anywhere.
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  What the module covers: H&Ps (99221–99223), progress notes (99231–99233), and consults (99252–99255) only. Discharge codes (99238/99239) are time-based, not MDM-based — exclude them entirely.              
   
  Charge volume formula: For each admission, count 1 H&P + (LOS − 2) progress notes (minimum 1) + consult count. LOS comes from a new input on the baseline page. Consults per admission are editable inside   
  the module (default 1.0).
