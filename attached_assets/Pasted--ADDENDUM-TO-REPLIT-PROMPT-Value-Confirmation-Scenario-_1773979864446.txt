 ADDENDUM TO REPLIT PROMPT: Value Confirmation + Scenario Modeling                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  This addendum extends the EBR redesign prompt. Read the previous prompt first.                                                                                                                               
  The core addition: the tool must answer one question every executive has —
  "How much is Abridge making or not making for me?" — with a clear confirmed                                                                                                                                  
  number AND a scenario model they can explore.
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━                                                                                                                                                                     
  THE EXPERIENCE ARC (revised)                                                                                                                                                                                 
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Screen 1 — Your Deployment        (context + go-live date + data source)
  Screen 2 — What Your Data Shows   (retrospective: confirmed value from actual data)                                                                                                                          
  Screen 3 — What You Could Earn    (prospective: scenario modeling, user-editable)
  Screen 4 — Where You're Going     (maturity stage + deepen/expand)                                                                                                                                           
  Screen 5 — Executive Summary      (PDF-ready: confirmed + projected side by side)
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━                                                                                                                                                                     
  SCREEN 2 REVISION: "What Your Data Shows"                                                                                                                                                                    
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  This screen answers: "Based on what you've entered, here is the value                                                                                                                                        
  we can attribute to Abridge during your deployment period."                                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  This is the CONFIRMED number. It is traceable, defensible, and explicitly                                                                                                                                    
  labeled as such. It should feel like a finding, not a pitch.                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  HERO TILE — style: large, centered, dark background card (#1A1A1A)                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐                                                                                                                                           
    │  CONFIRMED VALUE — [monthsOnAbridge] MONTHS                  │                                                                                                                                           
    │                                                              │                                                                                                                                           
    │  $X – $Y / year                                             │                                                                                                                                            
    │  [data source badge]                                         │
    │                                                              │                                                                                                                                           
    │  Across [N] providers · [M] Abridge-documented encounters    │
    │  ≈ $[per-provider] per provider per year                     │                                                                                                                                           
    └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Below the hero: four domain rows (the cards from the previous prompt spec).                                                                                                                                  
  One row per domain. Muted row if domain is pre-signal for their phase.
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  IMPORTANT: Show the confirmed value with a range (low/high attribution                                                                                                                                       
  50–75%). Do not pick one number and assert it as fact. The range IS the                                                                                                                                      
  honesty. Label it: "conservative (50%) — typical (75%) attribution."                                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  BELOW the domain rows: a single transparent formula block (always visible,                                                                                                                                   
  not hidden in an accordion):                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐                                                                                                                                           
    │  HOW WE CALCULATED THIS                                      │                                                                                                                                           
    │  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  │                                                                                                                                            
    │  [N] encounters × [X] min saved × $[rate]/hr                │
    │  + [Y] wRVU lift × [N] encounters × $[CF] × 50–75%         │                                                                                                                                             
    │  = $[low] – $[high] / year                                   │                                                                                                                                           
    │                                                              │                                                                                                                                           
    │  Deployment period: [go-live date] → today ([X] months)     │                                                                                                                                            
    │  Data source: [source badge]                                 │                                                                                                                                           
    └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  This block is always open. Not an accordion. The CFO will ask.                                                                                                                                               
  Have the answer ready.

  SCREEN 3 (NEW): "What You Could Earn"                                                                                                                                                                        
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  This is a NEW screen inserted between "What Your Data Shows" and                                                                                                                                             
  "Where You're Going." It is the prospective half of the EBR.                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  This screen answers: "Based on your data and these assumptions,                                                                                                                                              
  here is what Abridge could represent for your organization."                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  There are TWO sections on this screen:                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ─────────────────────────────────────────                                                                                                                                                                    
  SECTION A: "Your Scenarios"                                                                                                                                                                                  
  ─────────────────────────────────────────                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Show three scenario cards side by side (or stacked on mobile):
  CONSERVATIVE · TYPICAL · OPTIMISTIC                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Each card shows what the annual value would be at a specific                                                                                                                                                 
  adoption rate and value-per-encounter assumption.                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  DEFAULT scenario assumptions:                                                                                                                                                                                
    CONSERVATIVE: current utilization rate, 50% attribution
    TYPICAL:      75% utilization rate, 62% attribution (midpoint)                                                                                                                                             
    OPTIMISTIC:   90% utilization rate, 75% attribution     
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Each scenario card:                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    ┌─────────────────────────────────┐                                                                                                                                                                        
    │  CONSERVATIVE                   │                                                                                                                                                                        
    │                                 │                     
    │  $X / year                      │                                                                                                                                                                        
    │                                 │
    │  [N] providers                  │                                                                                                                                                                        
    │  [current]% adoption            │                     
    │  50% attribution                │                                                                                                                                                                        
    │                                 │                     
    │  [Edit assumptions ↓]           │                                                                                                                                                                        
    └─────────────────────────────────┘                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The TYPICAL card should be visually highlighted (brand orange border,                                                                                                                                        
  slightly larger) — it is Abridge's recommended anchor scenario.                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The CURRENT CONFIRMED VALUE appears above all three scenarios as a                                                                                                                                           
  reference line:                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    ← Your confirmed value: $X – $Y / year                                                                                                                                                                     
    ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────                                                                                                                                                   
    CONSERVATIVE    TYPICAL (recommended)    OPTIMISTIC                                                                                                                                                        
                                                            
  This framing makes the confirmed value the floor and the scenarios the                                                                                                                                       
  ceiling — which is the honest, defensible story.                                                                                                                                                             
                                              
  EDITABLE ASSUMPTIONS (per-scenario expand):                                                                                                                                                                  
  Each scenario card has an "Edit assumptions" toggle that reveals:
    - Adoption rate (slider, 10%–100%)                                                                                                                                                                         
    - Attribution rate (slider, 30%–80%, labeled "what % do we credit Abridge?")                                                                                                                               
    - Providers in scope (number input — defaults to totalProviders)                                                                                                                                           
    - Annual encounters per provider (defaults to derived from current data)                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  When a user edits any assumption, that scenario card gets a small badge:                                                                                                                                     
  "Custom" — distinguishing it from Abridge's default scenario.                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  This lets the CSM and partner explore together: "what if we get to                                                                                                                                           
  80% adoption? what if we added your cardiology group?" — live, in the room.                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ─────────────────────────────────────────                                                                                                                                                                    
  SECTION B: "The Gap"                                                                                                                                                                                         
  ─────────────────────────────────────────                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  One card below the scenarios. This is the most important single number                                                                                                                                       
  on this screen.                                                                                                                                                                                              
                                                            
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐                                                                                                                                           
    │  THE GAP                                                     │
    │                                                              │                                                                                                                                           
    │  The difference between your confirmed value and the         │                                                                                                                                           
    │  Typical scenario:                                           │
    │                                                              │                                                                                                                                           
    │  $[Typical - Confirmed Low] – $[Typical - Confirmed High]    │
    │  / year currently uncaptured                                 │                                                                                                                                           
    │                                                              │
    │  This gap closes primarily by:                               │                                                                                                                                           
    │  [1] Deepening adoption (you're at X%, target is 75%)        │                                                                                                                                           
    │  [2] [Domain with no data yet — e.g., "Measuring Capacity"]  │                                                                                                                                           
    │  [3] [If multi-setting opportunity: "Expanding to inpatient"] │                                                                                                                                          
    └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Style: cream/beige background (#F5F0EB), orange left border.                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The "gap closes primarily by" list is computed dynamically:                                                                                                                                                  
    - If utilizationRate < 60: always list "Deepen adoption" first                                                                                                                                             
    - If a domain is pre-signal or no-data: list "Measure [domain]"                                                                                                                                            
    - If totalProviders > providers: list "Expand to [N] additional providers"                                                                                                                                 
    - If care settings < available: list "Add [setting]"    
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Maximum 3 items. In order of estimated dollar impact.                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ─────────────────────────────────────────                                                                                                                                                                    
  WHAT THIS SCREEN IS NOT                                                                                                                                                                                      
  ─────────────────────────────────────────                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  This screen is NOT a projection that Abridge guarantees.  
  Add a single disclaimer line at the bottom (small, gray):                                                                                                                                                    
                                                            
  "Scenarios are modeled estimates based on your deployment data and                                                                                                                                           
  Abridge methodology. They represent what organizations at your scale
  typically see — not a guarantee. Conservative attribution (50%) is                                                                                                                                           
  used as the floor in all scenarios."

    Domain breakdown rows (4 domains)                                                                                                                                                                          
    Data source badge                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  SCENARIO SECTION (right column or below):

  The two sections should be visually distinct:                                                                                                                                                                
    Confirmed = dark tile (#1A1A1A background) — this is real                                                                                                                                                  
    Scenarios = cream tile (#F5F0EB background) — this is modeled                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  This visual split is the core design decision. The executive sees                                                                                                                                            
  both in one glance and understands immediately: "this is what happened,                                                                                                                                      
  this is what could happen."

  SCENARIO CALCULATION LOGIC                                                                                                                                                                                   
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Add to measureCalculator.ts:                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  export interface ScenarioInputs {                                                                                                                                                                            
    adoptionRate: number       // 0–100                                                                                                                                                                        
    attributionRate: number    // 0–100 (50 = conservative, 75 = typical)                                                                                                                                      
    providers: number                                                                                                                                                                                          
    encountersPerProvider: number             
  }                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  export interface ScenarioResult {                                                                                                                                                                            
    label: 'conservative' | 'typical' | 'optimistic' | 'custom'                                                                                                                                                
    inputs: ScenarioInputs                                                                                                                                                                                     
    annualValueLow: number                                                                                                                                                                                     
    annualValueHigh: number                                                                                                                                                                                    
    hoursReclaimed: number                                                                                                                                                                                     
    perProviderValue: number                                                                                                                                                                                   
    isCustomized: boolean                                                                                                                                                                                      
  }                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  export function calculateScenario(                                                                                                                                                                           
    state: MeasureState,                                                                                                                                                                                       
    inputs: ScenarioInputs,                                                                                                                                                                                    
    label: ScenarioResult['label']                                                                                                                                                                             
  ): ScenarioResult                                         
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Default scenario inputs (derived from state):                                                                                                                                                                
                                          
    function getDefaultScenarios(state: MeasureState): {                                                                                                                                                       
      conservative: ScenarioInputs                                                                                                                                                                             
      typical: ScenarioInputs                                                                                                                                                                                  
      optimistic: ScenarioInputs                                                                                                                                                                               
    } {                                                     
      const baseEncounters = state.deployment.totalEncounters > 0                                                                                                                                              
        ? state.deployment.totalEncounters / Math.max(state.deployment.providers, 1)                                                                                                                           
        : 200                                                                                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
      return {                                                                                                                                                                                                 
        conservative: {                                                                                                                                                                                        
          adoptionRate: state.deployment.utilizationRate,                                                                                                                                                      
          attributionRate: 50,                                                                                                                                                                                 
          providers: state.deployment.providers,
          encountersPerProvider: baseEncounters,                                                                                                                                                               
        },                                                                                                                                                                                                     
        typical: {                                                                                                                                                                                             
          adoptionRate: Math.min(75, Math.max(state.deployment.utilizationRate + 15, 60)),                                                                                                                     
          attributionRate: 62,                                                                                                                                                                                 
          providers: state.deployment.totalProviders || state.deployment.providers,                                                                                                                            
          encountersPerProvider: baseEncounters,                                                                                                                                                               
        },                                                                                                                                                                                                     
        optimistic: {                                                                                                                                                                                          
          adoptionRate: 90,                                                                                                                                                                                    
          attributionRate: 75,                              
          providers: state.deployment.totalProviders || state.deployment.providers,                                                                                                                            
          encountersPerProvider: baseEncounters,                                                                                                                                                               
        },                                                                                                                                                                                                     
      }                                                                                                                                                                                                        
    }

  TONE AND LANGUAGE GUIDE FOR NEW SCREENS 
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━    
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The tool speaks like a partner, not a vendor.
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    DO say:   "Your data shows..."                                                                                                                                                                             
    DON'T say: "Abridge has delivered..."                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    DO say:   "Based on your deployment, the math suggests..."                                                                                                                                                 
    DON'T say: "Abridge is generating $X in value for you"
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    DO say:   "The conservative scenario uses your current adoption rate."                                                                                                                                     
    DON'T say: "Even at conservative estimates, Abridge delivers..."                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    DO say:   "At the typical scenario, organizations your size find..."                                                                                                                                       
    DON'T say: "You could be making $X more"                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The confirmed number is a finding. The scenarios are a conversation.                                                                                                                                         
  The gap is an opportunity — never a failure.
