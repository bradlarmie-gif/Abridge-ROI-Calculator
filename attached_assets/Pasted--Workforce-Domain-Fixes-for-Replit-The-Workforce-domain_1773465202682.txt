# Workforce Domain — Fixes for Replit

The Workforce domain is the closest to being right. Levels 1-3 need minor fixes. Level 4 needs a rebuild. Keep the same UX patterns, card treatments, sidebar formula panel, and brand styling.

---

## Level 1: Time Is Returning — Minor fixes only

**Keep:** The after-hours input, the 46 clinical weeks, the Shanafelt citation, the pre-fill label explaining "Pre-filled from deployment benchmarks."

**Keep:** The formula and estimated impact output. This level works.

**No changes needed at Level 1.**

## Level 2: Burden Measured — Remove the burden score

**Keep:** The in-clinic time returned input (min/day), the clinician survey question (Not yet / Informal pulse / Structured survey), and the survey findings checkboxes.

**Remove:** The "Documentation burden score before" and "Documentation burden score after" fields (Scale of 1-10). These don't feed into any calculation and add dead weight to a 30-minute session. Cut both fields entirely.

**Everything else at Level 2 stays as-is.** The survey combo is strong — especially since Abridge has its own clinician survey tool. The in-clinic time savings formula is correct (min × providers × 230 / 60).

**Keep everything:**

## Level 4: REBUILD — Ambient as a Workforce Strategy

**Current state:** Called "Labor Line Impact." Asks for monthly agency/locum spend reduction and whether it's sustained. This is operational, not strategic. It doesn't feel like a meaningful step up from Level 3.

**New Level 4 name:** Workforce Strategically Managed

**New card text:**
```
Workforce Strategically Managed
Documentation burden reduction is a variable in
workforce strategy — recruitment, retention
programs, and staffing decisions.
```

**New description when selected:**
"Ambient documentation isn't just reducing burden — it's informing how your organization approaches workforce challenges. Recruitment, retention program design, staffing models, and provider experience strategy are connected to documentation burden data."

**New inputs:**

**Why this is the right Level 4:**

The jump from Level 3 to Level 4 is clear:
- Level 3: "We modeled turnover cost against burden." (Analysis)
- Level 4: "We use burden data to drive workforce strategy." (Action)

Level 3 is about understanding the exposure. Level 4 is about using that understanding to change how the organization approaches recruitment, retention, staffing, and provider experience. That's genuinely strategic.

The checkboxes at Level 4 are things that almost nobody is doing yet:
- Using ambient as a recruitment selling point
- Burden reduction as a measured retention intervention
- Time-to-fill connected to work environment improvements
- Documentation metrics in provider experience dashboards
- Staffing decisions informed by documentation efficiency

Most organizations will check 0-1. That gap is the conversation.

The agency/locum spend input is still here but it's ONE of several strategic indicators, not the entire level. It's a financial proof point within a broader strategic framework.
