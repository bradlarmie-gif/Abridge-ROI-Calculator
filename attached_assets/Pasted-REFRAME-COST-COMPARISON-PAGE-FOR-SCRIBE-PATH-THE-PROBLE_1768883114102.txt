REFRAME COST COMPARISON PAGE FOR SCRIBE PATH

## THE PROBLEM
We can't show Abridge pricing, so calling this "The cost comparison" 
is misleading. We need to reframe.

## THE FIX

Change the page from a "comparison" to a "context" page:

TITLE: "What you're spending today"
SUBTITLE: "Let's put your scribe investment in context."

REMOVE the side-by-side comparison layout.
REMOVE the "WITH ABRIDGE" card entirely.

INSTEAD, show ONE card with their scribe spend contextualized:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                 │
│  YOUR CURRENT SCRIBE PROGRAM                                    │
│                                                                 │
│  $300,000/year                                                  │
│                                                                 │
│  12 scribes × $25/hr × 20 hrs × 50 weeks                        │
│  Covering 12 of 30 providers (40%)                              │
│                                                                 │

│                                                                 │
│  BREAKING IT DOWN:                                              │
│                                                                 │
│  $25,000 per month                                              │
│  $6,250 per covered provider per year                           │
│  $0 for the other 18 providers                                  │
│                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

BELOW THE CARD, add context text:
"Abridge pricing varies by deployment. Your rep can provide specifics.
For now, let's look at the full cost of your scribe program — 
including the costs that don't show up in the budget."

CTA: "See the hidden costs →"

## WHY THIS IS BETTER
- No false comparison
- Contextualizes their spend (per month, per provider)
- Sets up the "hidden costs" conversation naturally
- Honest about Abridge pricing being a separate conversation
