Header: IS YOUR DOCUMENTATION INFRASTRUCTURE READY FOR WHAT COMES NEXT?

How Risk Feeds the Final Score
Maturity level: 1-4, same as other domains. Contributes to overall score (avg × 25 = out of 100).
Dollar value for total:

Level 1: $0 (not measured)
Level 2: $0 (qualitative — coverage score only)
Level 3: Dollar value from hours saved (if provided), otherwise $0
Level 4: Strategic value (if provided), otherwise $0

This is intentionally conservative. Risk/infrastructure doesn't inflate the total with made-up numbers. The value of this domain is the maturity insight and the gap visualization (unchecked boxes), not a financial headline. It complements the hard numbers from Capacity, Revenue, and Workforce.

Why This Works

Every input is something someone can actually answer. "Are you monitoring documentation quality?" Yes/no. "Which workflows are connected?" Check the boxes. No one has to guess at audit exposure percentages.
The gap is visual. Unchecked boxes at Levels 3 and 4 show what they're NOT doing. That's more powerful than any dollar figure.
It's forward-looking. This isn't "what did ambient save you in compliance costs." It's "are you building the infrastructure for what's coming." That's the strategic conversation that differentiates this assessment from a calculator.
It's honest about what can be quantified. Levels 1-2 don't pretend to have dollar values. Level 3 has a clean hours-based calculation. Level 4 lets the user provide a number if they have one. No fabricated metrics anywhere.
It positions Abridge without attacking anyone. The implicit message: any ambient tool can make notes better (Level 1). The question is what you build on that foundation. The organization that's at Level 1 with any vendor should be asking "why aren't we at Level 3?"
