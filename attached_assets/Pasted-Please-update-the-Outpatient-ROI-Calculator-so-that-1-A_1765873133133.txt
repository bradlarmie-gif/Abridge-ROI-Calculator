Please update the Outpatient ROI Calculator so that:

1) A new "Cost per provider per month" input is added to Scenario Inputs and drives the Investment Per Year calculation, and

2) Strategic Driver card enable/disable state controls which levers are included in the waterfall, impact cards, and top-line metrics.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1) Scenario Inputs: add Cost per provider per month
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
In the left panel "Scenario Inputs" section (or create it if it does not yet exist), include the following inputs in this order:

   • Abridge Utilization (%)
   • Annual Outpatient Encounters (in scope)
   • Annual Outpatient Encounters (enterprise)
   • Providers in Scope
   • Enterprise Provider Count
   • Average Revenue per Encounter ($)
   • NEW: Cost per provider per month ($)

For the new field:
   - Label: "Cost per provider per month ($)"
   - Type: numeric input
   - This should represent the contracted Abridge subscription cost per provider, per month for Outpatient in this scenario.

Update the Investment logic:
   • Define Investment Per Year as:
       investmentPerYear = costPerProviderPerMonth * providersInScope * 12
   • Use this calculated `investmentPerYear` value for:
       - The "Investment Per Year" KPI at the top of the ROI Calculator
       - Net Value Created = Total Annual Benefit – Investment Per Year
       - ROI Multiple = Total Annual Benefit ÷ Investment Per Year
   • If there is an existing direct “Investment” manual input, either:
       - Remove it, or
       - Treat it as read-only and derive it entirely from Cost per provider per month and Providers in Scope.

Do not change how Utilization (%) is used in the benefits logic; this change is only about having cost per provider per month explicitly drive the investment side of the model.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2) Strategic Driver cards control inclusion in ROI
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
For the Strategic Driver cards in the left panel (Clinician Time Recovered, Overtime Reduction, Clinician Retention, HCC & Chronic Condition Capture, Level of Service Alignment, Documentation-Driven Denials):

   • Each card should have an `enabled` state (true/false) that toggles when the card is clicked.
   • When a card is Enabled:
       - It is visually in the "Enabled" state (full opacity, black border, “Enabled” pill, etc., per previous instructions).
       - Its associated lever is INCLUDED in:
           • The waterfall chart
           • The "Annual Impact by Lever" table or impact cards
           • The Total Annual Benefit calculation
           • The Total Clinician Hours Recovered (if that lever contributes time)
   • When a card is Disabled:
       - It is visually in the "Disabled" state (muted, greyed out).
       - Its associated lever is EXCLUDED from:
           • The waterfall chart (no bar for that lever)
           • The impact by lever table/cards
           • The Total Annual Benefit calculation
           • The Total Clinician Hours Recovered (no contribution from that lever)

Implementation details:
   • Use a single source of truth for lever state, e.g.:
       `strategicDrivers = [{ id, enabled, ... }]`
   • The waterfall chart and impact table should be rendered based only on drivers where `enabled === true`.
   • As the user toggles a driver, recalculate:
       - Total Annual Benefit
       - Net Value Created
       - ROI Multiple
       - Total Clinician Hours Recovered
       - Waterfall bars
       - Impact by lever values

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
