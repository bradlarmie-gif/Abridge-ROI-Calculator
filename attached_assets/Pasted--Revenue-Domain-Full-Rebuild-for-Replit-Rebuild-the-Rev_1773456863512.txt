# Revenue Domain — Full Rebuild for Replit

Rebuild the Revenue domain. The current implementation has formula issues flagged across multiple audits, inputs that executives won't have, and levels that don't represent a clear enough progression. Keep the same UX patterns, card treatments, sidebar formula panel, and brand styling. Change the content, inputs, and calculations.

---

## Domain Header

**Keep the existing header:**
WHAT IS DOCUMENTATION FIDELITY WORTH TO YOUR REVENUE CYCLE?

**Keep the existing subhead:**
Revenue cycle can only work with what documentation gives them. Every encounter is either capturing the revenue it earned — or leaking it.

**Add instruction above the level cards:**
"Select the level that best describes your organization today."

## Level 2: Directional Signal

**Card text:**
```
Directional Signal
Your organization has observed trends suggesting
documentation is affecting reimbursement.
Not yet formally validated.
```

**Description when selected:**
"Your organization has observed movement in coding, denials, or collections — but hasn't done a formal before/after analysis."

**Inputs:**
```
What has your organization observed?
☐ wRVU per encounter trending upward
☐ Coding specificity improving (ICD-10 distribution shifting)
☐ Denial rates trending downward
☐ Collections per encounter trending upward
☐ CDI query volume decreasing
☐ Coder productivity improving

What's your directional estimate of the revenue impact?
○ Under 1% of encounter revenue
○ 1-3% of encounter revenue
○ 3-5% of encounter revenue
○ 5%+ of encounter revenue
○ Not sure — we see movement but haven't estimated
```

**Remove from Level 2:**
- Remove the "How far along" timeline radio (under 30 days, 30-90, 90+) — not needed
- Remove the "Estimated coding specificity improvement %" input
- Remove the "Current denial rate %" input
- Remove the coding uplift formula entirely: `encounters × revenue × improvement % × capture rate` — this was flagged as the most misleading calculation in the tool across multiple audits. Gone.
- Remove the 0.5% CDI floor estimate
- Remove any dollar calculations that use benchmark percentages applied to encounter revenue

**Estimated Impact card:**

If a percentage range is selected (not "not sure"):
```
ESTIMATED IMPACT

$[lowEstimate]-$[highEstimate] directional estimate

Your organization estimates [selectedRange] of
encounter revenue is being affected by documentation
improvements.

At [documentedEncounters] documented encounters
× $[revenuePerVisit] per visit:
[lowPct]% = $[lowEstimate]
[highPct]% = $[highEstimate]

trending data. A formal before/after analysis
(Level 3) would validate this figure.

[checkedCount] area(s) showing movement:
[list of checked items]

Estimates based on your inputs. Individual results vary.
```

The dollar range calculation:
```
lowEstimate = documentedEncounters × revenuePerVisit × lowPct
highEstimate = documentedEncounters × revenuePerVisit × highPct

For "Under 1%": lowPct = 0.005, highPct = 0.01
For "1-3%": lowPct = 0.01, highPct = 0.03
For "3-5%": lowPct = 0.03, highPct = 0.05
For "5%+": lowPct = 0.05, highPct = 0.07
```

If "Not sure" selected:
```
ESTIMATED IMPACT

[checkedCount] area(s) showing movement

Your organization has observed changes in
[checkedCount] area(s):
[list of checked items]

Movement is visible but the impact hasn't been
estimated yet. A formal before/after analysis
(Level 3) would quantify it.

Estimates based on your inputs. Individual results vary.
```

**Key principle:** The dollar figure at Level 2 comes from THEIR estimate (the range they selected), not from a benchmark formula we built. We're just applying their estimate to their encounter volume. This is fundamentally different from the old approach which generated numbers from hidden assumptions.

## What Gets Removed From Revenue Entirely

1. **HCC / risk adjustment calculations.** All of them. The $200/member assumption, the VBC% input, the $8.51M output. Move HCC to Quality domain if appropriate or remove from the tool entirely. It was flagged as the most problematic number across all audits.

2. **The coding uplift formula at Level 2.** `encounters × revenue × coding improvement % × capture rate`. Methodologically wrong. Replaced with the user's own directional estimate.

3. **The 0.5% CDI floor estimate.** Benchmark-derived number at a level where the org hasn't measured. Removed.

4. **The word "upcoding."** Search the entire Revenue domain. Replace every instance with "coding accuracy opportunity."

5. **The "Documentation deficiency or query rate" input at Level 1.** An exec at Level 1 won't know this and we don't use it in a calculation.

6. **All auto-calculated dollar figures at Level 1.** No denial exposure, no wRVU opportunity, no projections from benchmarks. Level 1 = nobody has looked = no dollar figure.

7. **The VBC% input field.** Remove from Revenue. If VBC/HCC analysis belongs anywhere, it's in Quality.

## After Implementing

Browse through all four Revenue levels with test data (50 providers, 100K encounters, 60% utilization, $200/visit, $33 CMS factor) and verify:

1. Level 1 shows NO dollar figures — only encounter volume and "Not yet analyzed"
2. Level 2 shows a dollar range ONLY if the user selected an estimate range — and the range uses their selection, not a benchmark formula
3. Level 2 shows NO dollar figure if user selected "Not sure"
4. Level 3 shows dollars from the user's measured input — with the correct formula for each measurement type
5. Level 3 denial rate is qualitative only — no dollar conversion
6. Level 4 shows dollars only if the user entered an attributed revenue figure
7. The word "upcoding" does not appear anywhere
8. HCC, VBC%, and denial exposure calculations are completely gone from Level 1
9. The "No attribution discount applied to user-measured values" note appears at Level 3
10. All benchmark text includes "Based on aggregated deployment experience" where applicable
