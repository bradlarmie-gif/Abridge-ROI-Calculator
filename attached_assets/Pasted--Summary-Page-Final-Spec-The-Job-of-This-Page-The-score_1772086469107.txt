# Summary Page — Final Spec

## The Job of This Page

The score page was the mirror. The cost page was the weight. This page is the relief.

The executive is uncomfortable. They've seen their maturity gaps and the cost of inaction. Now they need to feel like there's a clear path forward — and that the person who built this assessment is the one who can help them walk it.

This page should take 60 seconds to read and leave them with two things: a roadmap they can reference tomorrow, and a reason to have a deeper conversation.

---

## Layout

Same two-column layout. Same card treatments. Same everything.

### Hero Section (dark card, full width — same treatment as the assessment narrative on the score page)

```
AMBIENT ASSESSMENT

    [score]                      $[totalMeasured]
    / 100                        annually

[maturity bar with position marker]
Early deployment    Measured    Strategically managed    Best in class

```

This is a COMPACT recap — not a repeat of the score page. One dark card, score on the left, measured value on the right, one-line narrative below. The executive glances at this and remembers where they are. Takes 3 seconds.

**Dynamic one-line summary:**
```
0-30:  "in the early stages of capturing ambient ROI"
31-50: "beginning to measure ambient ROI but significant opportunity remains"
51-70: "actively managing ambient ROI with room to deepen"
71-85: "strategically managing ambient ROI across most domains"
86+:   "operating at best-in-class documentation intelligence"
```

Same warm gray card. This is the closing.

```
NEXT STEP

INTELLIGENCE LOOKS LIKE AT YOUR SCALE?

This is not a product demonstration. It is a
strategic working session where we walk through
your organization's specific opportunities across

[Request a Working Session →]     [Export My Assessment]
```

Two buttons side by side:
- **"Request a Working Session →"** — red/Abridge CTA button (primary). Links to scheduling or a form.
- **"Export My Assessment"** — outlined/secondary button. Generates a PDF or shareable document with their score, domain breakdown, roadmap, and assessment details.

The language "strategic working session" is intentional. Not a demo. Not a sales call. A working session. That's what earns the meeting.

"This is not a product demonstration" is the line that builds trust. It says: we're not going to pitch you. We're going to work.

## What the Export PDF Should Contain

When they click "Export My Assessment," generate a clean document with:

1. **Header:** Ambient Assessment — [Date]
2. **Score:** [X] / 100 with maturity band
3. **Domain breakdown:** 4 domains with level, score, and measured value
4. **Strategic roadmap:** The 4 domain blocks from Card 1 (current level + next step)
5. **Gap summary:** Deployment reality table (their numbers vs. benchmark)
6. **Baseline inputs:** All their entered values
7. **Disclaimer**

No cost-of-waiting numbers in the export. No aggressive financial projections. The export should feel like a strategic assessment document, not a sales pitch. When the executive forwards it to their COO, it should read like something a consultant produced — thoughtful, specific, and credible.

---

## What This Page Does NOT Have

- No repeat of the full score page (just a compact hero recap)
- No repeat of the cost page graph or cost-of-waiting numbers
- No "highest-leverage opportunity" callout with a big dollar figure (that was the cost page)
- No aggressive language about what they're losing
- No product features or competitive positioning

## What This Page DOES Have

- A compact hero reminding them of their score and measured value (3 seconds)
- A strategic roadmap with specific, credible next steps per domain (60 seconds to read)
- Their assessment details for reference (when they share it)
- A next step that feels like a strategic partnership, not a sales funnel
- An export they can share without embarrassment
