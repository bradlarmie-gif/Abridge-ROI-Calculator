**Card 2: ASSESSMENT**

Same warm gray card. Below the score card.

```
ASSESSMENT

Actively managing in some areas. Significant opportunity remains.

Your organization is actively managing ambient ROI in Capacity
and Workforce, but Revenue and Risk remain in early stages.
Your biggest opportunity is in Revenue — no one has connected
documentation quality to how your organization gets paid.
```

- First line bold (the headline assessment)
- Body text in regular weight gray, same as description text throughout the tool
- 2-3 sentences max

**Dynamic headline logic:**
```
Score 0-30:   "Early stages. Significant opportunity across all domains."
Score 31-50:  "Emerging awareness. Key domains remain unmeasured."
Score 51-70:  "Actively managing in some areas. Significant opportunity remains."
Score 71-85:  "Strategically managed. Closing the final gaps."
Score 86-100: "Best in class across domains."
```

**Dynamic body text logic:**

Names strong domains (Level 3+) and weak domains (Level 1-2). Always names the single lowest domain as "biggest opportunity" with a one-line domain-specific insight.

Domain-specific insight lines:
```
Capacity L1: "Recovered time isn't being tracked or deployed."
Capacity L2: "Recovered time is measured but not being converted to access."
Revenue L1:  "No one has connected documentation quality to how your organization gets paid."
Revenue L2:  "Revenue signals observed but not measured."
Workforce L1: "After-hours burden reduced but broader workforce impact isn't tracked."
Workforce L2: "Burden is measured but not connected to retention or labor costs."
Risk L1:     "Documentation quality improved but nothing downstream has changed."
Risk L2:     "Quality monitoring started but downstream workflows aren't connected."
```

Tiebreaker order if domains have the same score: Risk > Revenue > Workforce > Capacity.

**Methodology link:**

Below the assessment card, small gray text:
*"How is this calculated?"*

Expands inline (same accordion/expand pattern if used elsewhere in the tool, or just appears as a paragraph below):

> Each domain contributes up to 25 points based on the maturity level you selected. Level 1 = 6 pts, Level 2 = 12 pts, Level 3 = 19 pts, Level 4 = 25 pts. Your total score reflects how intentionally your organization is managing the value created by ambient documentation.

## Score Calculation

```javascript
const SCORE_MAP = { 1: 6, 2: 12, 3: 19, 4: 25 };

const totalScore =
  SCORE_MAP[capacityLevel] +
  SCORE_MAP[revenueLevel] +
  SCORE_MAP[workforceLevel] +
  SCORE_MAP[riskLevel];
```

Score ranges:
- All Level 1s = 24
- All Level 2s = 48
- All Level 3s = 76
- All Level 4s = 100
- Typical org (mix of L1-L2) = 30-48
