──────────────────
Ambient AI value is multiplicative, not additive. Small gaps in each 
dimension compound into large overall gaps:

- If you're at 60% of utilization potential
- And 50% of efficiency potential  
- And 40% of quality potential
- And 76% of satisfaction potential

You're not at (60+50+40+76)/4 = 57% of total value.
You're capturing the interaction of all four — and gaps compound.

THE FOUR DIMENSIONS EXPLAINED
─────────────────────────────
UTILIZATION: What % of encounters actually get documented with the tool?
→ Low utilization = you're paying for capacity you're not using

EFFICIENCY: How much time does it actually save per encounter?
→ Low efficiency = the tool isn't integrated into workflow properly

QUALITY: Is documentation improving coding accuracy and revenue capture?
→ Low quality = you're missing the revenue optimization opportunity

SATISFACTION: Do providers actually like using it?
→ Low satisfaction = utilization will decline over time
