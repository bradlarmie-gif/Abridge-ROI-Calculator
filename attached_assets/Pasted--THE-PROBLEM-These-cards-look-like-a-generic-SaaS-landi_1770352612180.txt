## THE PROBLEM

These cards look like a generic SaaS landing page. Three boxes, three buttons, done. There's nothing wrong with them per se—they're clean, they're readable—but they don't create **energy.** They don't make someone lean in.

The issue isn't the content. It's that all three cards have **identical visual weight.** Same size, same shape, same button, same everything. Your eye scans across them and goes "okay, three options" and that's it. No pull. No depth. No sense that each path is a meaningfully different experience.

---

## WHAT "INDUSTRY LEADING" ACTUALLY LOOKS LIKE HERE

Think about what this page needs to do. Someone lands here and within 3 seconds needs to:

1. Understand this is something different (not another calculator)
2. Feel which lane is THEIRS
3. Want to click

Right now it's doing #1 okay (the headline helps) but failing at #2 and #3.

---

Each path is a fundamentally different experience. They should FEEL different.
