GLOBAL ISSUES ACROSS ALL PAGES:

Step indicator pills (1 Input → 2 Gap → 3 Proof, etc.) are too prominent and use wrong colors. Should be subtle, gray, with only current step highlighted.
Red/orange label text above headlines ("CURRENT STATE," "PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS," etc.) is competing with the headlines. Should be smaller, grayer, quieter.
Dark banners (black backgrounds) don't match Abridge brand. Replace with warm beige or white with red-orange accents.
Slider inputs with red dots feel off-brand. Should be cleaner, more minimal.
"Benchmark" comparisons use red text that feels like warnings. Should be neutral or use red-orange sparingly.
Headlines are good but some subheads are too wordy or try too hard.
CTAs at bottom need consistency—some are black buttons, some are styled differently.
