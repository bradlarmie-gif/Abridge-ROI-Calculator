INPATIENT DRIVERS (in this order):

TIME SAVED BENEFITS:

1. ROUNDING EFFICIENCY & TIME SAVINGS
   Subtitle: "Give hospitalists their time back"
   
   The Theory:
   "Hospitalists spend 2+ hours per day on documentation—much of it after rounds or at home. Abridge captures notes in real-time at the bedside, returning that time to patient care, teaching, discharge planning, or personal life."
   
   How We Calculate It:
   
   STEP 1: DOCUMENTATION BURDEN
   "How much time is spent documenting?"
   20 hospitalists × 2.5 hrs/day × 250 days = 12,500 hours/year
   
   STEP 2: TIME RETURNED
   "How much can Abridge give back?"
   12,500 hrs × 40% reduction × 65% adoption = 3,250 hours returned
   
   STEP 3: VALUE OF TIME
   "What's that time worth?"
   3,250 hours × $75/hr opportunity cost = $243,750
   
   Note: "This is 'time value'—it doesn't directly generate revenue, but it affects retention, quality of life, and capacity for complex cases."

2. HOSPITALIST RETENTION
   Subtitle: "Address the #1 driver of hospitalist turnover"
   
   The Theory:
   "Hospitalist programs face a retention crisis—turnover rates of 15-20% are common. Documentation burden is the #1 cited frustration. Replacing a hospitalist costs $400-600K."
   
   How We Calculate It:
   
   STEP 1: BASELINE TURNOVER
   "What's the current situation?"
   20 hospitalists × 15% turnover = 3.0 departures/year
   
   STEP 2: BURNOUT-RELATED
   "How much is burnout-driven?"
   3.0 × 60% burnout-related = 1.8 preventable departures
   
   STEP 3: DOCUMENTATION ATTRIBUTION
   "How much is documentation's fault?"
   1.8 × 35% documentation-driven = 0.63 departures
   
   STEP 4: ABRIDGE IMPACT
   "What can Abridge prevent?"
   0.63 × 50% = 0.32 departures avoided
   
   STEP 5: COST SAVINGS
   "What's the dollar value?"
   0.32 × $500,000 = $160,000
   
   Note: "⚠️ Hospitalist turnover is significantly higher than other specialties. Retention impact measurable after 12-18 months."

---

DOCUMENTATION QUALITY BENEFITS:

1. CC/MCC CAPTURE (DRG OPTIMIZATION)
   Subtitle: "Document the complexity you're managing"
   
   The Theory:
   "DRG reimbursement depends on documented comorbidities. Conditions discussed at bedside but not captured in notes mean missed CC/MCC assignments and lower DRG weights. Abridge ensures what's discussed gets documented."
   
   How We Calculate It:
   
   STEP 1: ADMISSIONS WITH OPPORTUNITY
   "How many admissions have documentation gaps?"
   5,200 documented admissions × 40% with gaps = 2,080 opportunities
   
   STEP 2: CAPTURE IMPROVEMENT
   "How much can Abridge help?"
   2,080 × 15% improvement = 312 admissions improved
   
   STEP 3: DRG WEIGHT IMPACT
   "What's the revenue impact?"
   312 × 0.4 weight increase × $6,000 = $748,800
   
   STEP 4: REALITY CHECK
   "What passes audit?"
   $748,800 × 50% realization = $374,400
   
   Note: "Work with your CDI team to validate capture rates for your specific case mix."

2. CDI QUERY REDUCTION
   Subtitle: "Better initial documentation = less rework"
   
   The Theory:
   "CDI teams spend enormous effort querying physicians for clarification. Better initial documentation reduces query volume—saving CDI time and reducing physician interruptions."
   
   How We Calculate It:
   
   STEP 1: CURRENT QUERY VOLUME
   "How many queries happen today?"
   8,000 admissions × 30% query rate = 2,400 queries/year
   
   STEP 2: QUERIES AVOIDED
   "How many can better documentation prevent?"
   2,400 × 25% reduction = 600 queries avoided
   
   STEP 3: VALUE
   "What's the operational savings?"
   600 queries × $50/query = $30,000
   
   Note: "Additional benefit: Faster DRG finalization → faster billing cycles"

3. DOCUMENTATION-RELATED DENIALS
   Subtitle: "Protect your reimbursement"
   
   The Theory:
   "Inpatient claims face rigorous payer review. Medical necessity, level of care, and clinical indicators must be clearly documented. Incomplete notes lead to costly denials and appeals."
   
   How We Calculate It:
   
   STEP 1: DENIAL VOLUME
   "How many claims are denied?"
   8,000 admissions × 5% denial rate = 400 denials
   
   STEP 2: DOCUMENTATION-RELATED
   "How many are doc-related?"
   400 × 35% = 140 doc-related denials
   
   STEP 3: PREVENTION
   "How many can better documentation prevent?"
   140 × 40% = 56 denials prevented
   
   STEP 4: VALUE
   "What's the revenue impact?"
   56 × $12,000 avg claim = $672,000
   
   STEP 5: CONSERVATIVE ADJUSTMENT
   "What's the permanent save rate?"
   $672,000 × 40% = $268,800
   
   Note: "Many denials are eventually overturned on appeal—this counts only permanent saves."
