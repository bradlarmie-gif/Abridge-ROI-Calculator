  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════                                                                                                                                                  
  File: client/src/lib/measureCareSettings.ts (lines ~3-20)
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Add four new fields to the MetricDefinition interface. Keep all existing fields unchanged.
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    roiQuadrant: 'Capacity' | 'Workforce' | 'Revenue' | 'Quality';
    shortDescription: string;        // 1-2 sentence neutral narrative for Explore toggle expansion. May be empty string for now — we'll populate in a follow-up sprint.                                       
    exploreVisibility: 'driver-quantified' | 'driver-qualitative' | 'hidden';
    childOfDriver?: string;           // Optional. Marks this measure as a sub-toggle of another driver. Example: locum spend is childOfDriver: 'retention' so the UI nests it under the Retention toggle.     
                                                             
  Why these fields:                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - roiQuadrant: canonical grouping for the 4-quadrant Explore restructure.
  - shortDescription: replaces the use of `whyItMatters` for the Explore tooltip/expansion. Keeps voice tight and consistent.                                                                                  
  - exploreVisibility: 3 states. 'driver-quantified' = appears as toggle WITH dollar math + scenario presets. 'driver-qualitative' = appears as toggle, narrative only, no dollar math. 'hidden' = does NOT    
  appear in Explore (still in Measure post-sale catalog). 
  - childOfDriver: lets the UI render Locum/Agency as a nested sub-toggle under Retention/Wellbeing.
