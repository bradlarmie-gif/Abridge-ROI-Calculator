Please add a new chart under the ROI Waterfall called “Enterprise Expansion View” that compares the current in-scope scenario to a full enterprise rollout.

SECTION & COPY
──────────────
Insert a new section directly under the ROI Waterfall:

• Section title: "Enterprise Expansion View"
• Subtitle (muted): "Comparing impact for this in-scope cohort vs a full enterprise rollout."

CHART TYPE
──────────
Use a grouped column chart:

• X-axis categories: one category for each *active* lever, plus a final category:
    - Clinician Time Recovered (if active)
    - Overtime Reduction (if active)
    - Clinician Retention (if active)
    - HCC & Chronic Condition Capture (if active)
    - Level of Service Alignment (if active)
    - Documentation-Driven Denials (if active)
    - Total Annual Benefit (always shown)

• For each category, render two bars:
    1) Current Scenario (in-scope)
    2) Enterprise Projection

COLOR & STYLE
─────────────
• Current Scenario bar:
    - Solid muted green (same family as benefit bars in the waterfall).
• Enterprise Projection bar:
    - Same hue, but lighter shade / ~50% opacity, OR
    - White fill with a green outline.
• Light gray axis lines, small neutral labels.
• Legend at top-left of the chart:
    - Solid bar label: "Current scenario (in-scope)"
    - Outlined / lighter bar label: "Enterprise projection"

CALCULATION LOGIC
──────────────────
We already calculate a dollar value per lever for the current in-scope group (what displays in the Annual Impact by Lever table). Reuse that value and scale it to enterprise using the existing enterprise inputs on the left panel.

Inputs available:
• Annual Outpatient Encounters (in scope)
• Annual Outpatient Encounters (enterprise)
• Providers in Scope
• Enterprise Provider Count
• Any utilization % we already apply for encounters in scope.

Implementation guidance:
1) For each active lever, compute a per-encounter benefit based on the in-scope group:
      perEncounterBenefit = scopeLeverValue / max(1, scopeEncounterCount)

2) Compute the enterprise version by scaling that per-encounter benefit:
      enterpriseLeverValue = perEncounterBenefit * enterpriseEncounterCount

   (Use the "Annual Outpatient Encounters (enterprise)" field for enterpriseEncounterCount.)

3) For "Total Annual Benefit":
   • Current scenario total = sum of all active lever values (we already have this).
   • Enterprise total = sum of all enterpriseLeverValue values.

4) If a lever is disabled in the UI:
   • Do not include it as a category on the x-axis.
   • Exclude its value from both the current and enterprise totals.

INTERACTION
───────────
• Hovering a bar should show a tooltip:
    - Lever name or "Total Annual Benefit"
    - Scenario type: "Current scenario" or "Enterprise projection"
    - Dollar value (e.g., "$1,150,000")

• The chart should update live whenever:
    - Scenario inputs change (providers in scope, enterprise providers, encounters, etc.)
    - A strategic driver is enabled/disabled.

SCOPE
─────
• Do NOT change the existing ROI Waterfall or lever math.
• Only add this new chart and any necessary calculations to support it.
• Keep styling consistent with the rest of the app (same fonts, spacing, and neutral colors).
