2. SCRIBE COST REDUCTION
   Subtitle: "Convert labor cost to technology investment"
   
   The Theory:
   "Many EDs rely on scribes to handle documentation burden. Abridge can reduce scribe needs or eliminate them entirely—freeing up significant labor budget."

   STEP 1: CURRENT SCRIBE INVESTMENT
   "What are you spending on scribes?"

   "How many can Abridge replace?"

   "What's the budget impact?"
   7.5 FTEs × $45,000 = $337,500 annual savings
   
   Note: "Some organizations redeploy scribes rather than eliminate—still creates budget flexibility"

   STEP 4: COST SAVINGS
   "What's the dollar value?"
   0.30 × $800,000 replacement cost = $240,000
