MEASURE PATH — THE SOUL VERSION
THE STORY WE'RE TELLING
This is a tool for validation and celebration. The client already chose Abridge. They already went through implementation. They already lived the change.
Now they need to:

See the change reflected back to them in data
Understand what it means
Feel proud of what they accomplished
Take a story back to their team that's credible and compelling

The emotional arc:
Page 1: "Let's look at your results" (anticipation)
Page 2: "Here's what you measured" (grounding)
Page 3: "Look what changed" (wow)
Page 4: "Here's what we can prove—and here's how to think about the rest" (clarity)
Page 5: "Here's your story to tell" (empowerment)

THE CORE PHILOSOPHY
What we measure, we state.
What we can't measure, we frame.
We don't inflate. We don't guess. We show what's real, then help them think about what it means.

THE MATH (Final Version)
Time Reclaimed:
Copy(Before time - After time) × Total Encounters ÷ 60 = Hours
Revenue Captured (wRVU):
CopywRVU Lift × Documented Encounters × $33 × Attribution %
- Documented Encounters = Total Encounters × Utilization Rate
- Default Attribution: 50% (show range at 50-75%)
Capacity (Optional, user-driven):
CopyHours × Capacity % × (60 ÷ Visit Duration) × Revenue per Visit
- User inputs: Capacity %, Visit Duration, Revenue per Visit
- Defaults: 0%, 30 min, $200
Cost Reduction (Optional, user-driven):
CopyDirect input from user
- No calculation—they tell us what they saved
Wellbeing (No dollar figure):
CopyShow: Hours per week back per provider
Context: "1 provider retained = $300-500K"
