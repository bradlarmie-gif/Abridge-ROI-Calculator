MEASURE PATH — COMPREHENSIVE REPLIT PROMPT
THE OBJECTIVE
An existing Abridge customer needs to:

Enter their before/after data
See what changed (visually)
Understand the financial value of those changes
Get a boardroom-ready story they can share internally

This is a celebration and validation tool. The client should leave feeling confident that Abridge is working and equipped to tell that story to their leadership.

PAGE 4: THE VALUE
Purpose:
Translate the changes into financial impact. Show all value categories—no story selector needed.
The Math:
Time Value Calculation:
CopyHours Saved = (Before time - After time) × Total Encounters ÷ 60

Example: (12 - 4) × 47,000 ÷ 60 = 6,267 hours
Time Value Allocation (defaults, adjustable):
CopyCapacity Value:
  Hours toward capacity = Hours Saved × 20%
  Additional visits = Hours toward capacity × (60 ÷ 30 min per visit)
  Revenue = Additional visits × $200 per visit

Savings Value:
  Hours toward savings = Hours Saved × 50%
  Direct savings = Hours toward savings × $150 OT rate

Wellbeing Value:
  Hours toward wellbeing = Hours Saved × 30%
  Hours back per provider per week = (Hours toward wellbeing ÷ Providers) ÷ (Months × 4.33 weeks)
  Note: "1 provider retained = $300-500K saved"
Documentation Value Calculation:
CopywRVU Lift = After wRVU - Before wRVU
Documented Encounters = Total Encounters × Utilization Rate
Additional wRVUs = wRVU Lift × Documented Encounters
Revenue = Additional wRVUs × $33 (Medicare conversion factor) × Attribution % (default 50%)

Example: 0.20 × 33,840 × $33 × 50% = $111,672
Total Annual Value:
CopyTotal = Capacity Value + Savings Value + Documentation Value

WHAT'S DIFFERENT FROM BEFORE

No story selector. We calculate all three value categories and show them. The math does the work.
Clear math with adjustable inputs. Users can see and modify assumptions if they want, but defaults are sensible.
Specific financial assumptions. $33 wRVU (Medicare), $200/visit, $150/hr OT—grounded in reality.
Lead with human story on final page. "You gave 80 people their evenings back" is the headline, not a footnote.
Premium Abridge aesthetic. Warm beige, red-orange accents, big hero numbers, generous whitespace.
Expansion opportunity is subtle. "What if you expanded?" doesn't compete with the main story.
