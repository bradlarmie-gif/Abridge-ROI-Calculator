## Issue 1: Remove Confusing Weights from Score Calculation

The current weighted score (30%, 30%, 25%, 15%) is confusing and arbitrary.

Replace with a simple average:

```javascript

  (utilizationScore * 0.30) +
  (efficiencyScore * 0.30) +
  (qualityScore * 0.25) +
  (satisfactionScore * 0.15)
);

// NEW (simple average)

  (utilizationScore + efficiencyScore + qualityScore + satisfactionScore) / 4
