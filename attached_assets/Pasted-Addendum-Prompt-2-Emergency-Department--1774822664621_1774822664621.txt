  New mechanism card — Admission Capture                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Title: "Admission Capture"                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Inside the card:                                                                                                                                                                                           

  Mechanism paragraph:
  Of the patients recovered from LWBS, some will require inpatient admission. When those patients are retained rather than leaving, the ED visit converts to inpatient admission revenue — DRG-based payment on
   top of the ED facility and professional fees. This driver only activates when LWBS Recovery is calculated first; the recovered patient population is the base.
                                          
  Formula block (use existing style):
  Recovered LWBS patients × admission rate % × avg admission revenue × realization rate = admission capture value                                                                                              
     
  Key assumptions list:                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Admission rate from recovered LWBS patients: 15–25% (patients who left without being seen and required inpatient care)                                                                                   
  - Average admission revenue: $8,000–$12,000 (DRG-based; varies by payer mix and case mix)                               
  - Realization rate: 70–80% — accounts for payer mix, bed availability, and coding factors
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  "Why this is defensible" callout (orange left border):                                                                                                                                                       
  ED admission rates are tracked in your operational data. The recovered patient population comes directly from the LWBS calculation — it's not a new assumption, it's an extension of the same patient cohort.
   The admission revenue figure uses your actual DRG base rate, not a benchmark.                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Add an important dependency note:                                                                                                                                                                            
  This calculation depends on LWBS Recovery. If you don't have LWBS data, don't estimate admission capture — the compounding of two estimates produces a number that won't survive scrutiny. Run LWBS first,   
  get the data, then layer in admission capture.
