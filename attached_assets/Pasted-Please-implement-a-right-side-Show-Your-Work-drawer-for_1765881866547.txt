3. ASSUMPTIONS (Editable Inputs)
────────────────────────────────────────

Use the same UI components as the Patient Access drawer.

(A) Hours available to reduce premium labor

1. **Realized clinician hours (annual)**  
   - Label: “Reclaimed clinician hours (annual)”  
   - Value: READ-ONLY  
   - Bind to the global “clinician hours recovered” for the in-scope cohort

6. **% of premium hours covered by locums**

────────────────────────────────────────
5. Calculation Logic (Bind to ROI Engine)
────────────────────────────────────────

Let:
    reclaimedHours = global annual clinician hours recovered (read-only)
    pctHoursPreviouslyPremium = slider (0–0.5)
    pctPremiumRealized = slider (0–1.0)
    overtimeRate
    locumRate
    locumShare = slider (0–1.0)
    overtimeShare = (1 - locumShare)

Compute:

1) premiumHoursExposed =
      reclaimedHours * pctHoursPreviouslyPremium

2) premiumHoursReduced =
      premiumHoursExposed * pctPremiumRealized

3) blendedRate =
      (locumShare * locumRate) +
      (overtimeShare * overtimeRate)

4) annualSavings =
      premiumHoursReduced * blendedRate

Bind this value to:
   • The Overtime & Locum Cost Avoidance lever’s annual impact
   • Total Annual Impact
   • Net Gain
   • Return (x)
   • Waterfall bar for this lever
   • Enterprise Expansion View scaling logic

────────────────────────────────────────
6. Formula Block (“How this is calculated”)
────────────────────────────────────────

Include this text in monospace formatting:

"Annual savings =
  Reclaimed clinician hours
  × % of hours that would have been overtime or locum
  × % of those hours realistically avoided
  × Blended premium hourly rate

Blended premium hourly rate =
  (Locum share × Locum hourly rate)
  + (Overtime share × Overtime hourly rate)"
