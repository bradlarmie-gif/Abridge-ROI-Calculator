REPLIT PROMPT: Measure Path EBR Redesign    
                                              
  You are redesigning the Measure path of The ROI Calculator to function as a             
  premium Executive Business Review (EBR) tool for existing Abridge partners.                                                                                                                                  
  The redesign must embed a specific strategic framework that Abridge uses                                                                                                                                     
  internally — four value domains, four maturity stages, and a phased time-to-value                                                                                                                            
  model. This is NOT a sales calculator. It's a structured partner review instrument.                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The full framework is defined below. Read it carefully — it shapes every screen.                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━                                                                                                                                                                     
  PART 1: THE FRAMEWORK TO EMBED                                                                                                                                                                               
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  FOUR VALUE DOMAINS (replace "doc quality" and "time value" buckets)
  Each domain answers one strategic question. Each has a stakeholder owner.                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    QUALITY      → "Has documentation quality traveled downstream?"                                                                                                                                            
                   Stakeholder: CMO, Quality & CDI                                                                                                                                                             
                   First signal: Day 1–30                                                                                                                                                                      
                   Metrics: note accuracy, CDI query rate, HCC capture,                                                                                                                                        
                            diagnosis specificity                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    WORKFORCE    → "Has clinician relief translated into tangible benefits?"                                                                                                                                   
                   Stakeholder: CHRO, CMO                                                                                                                                                                      
                   First signal: Day 30–90                                                                                                                                                                     
                   Metrics: time in notes, after-hours work (WoW),                                                                                                                                             
                            burnout indicators, retention                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    REVENUE      → "Has documentation quality reached the bottom line?"
                   Stakeholder: CFO, VP Revenue Cycle                                                                                                                                                          
                   First signal: Day 30–90                                                                                                                                                                     
                   Metrics: wRVU per encounter, E/M level accuracy,                                                                                                                                            
                            coding accuracy, clean claim rate, CMI (inpatient)                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    CAPACITY     → "What is the organization doing with the freed time?"
    (or PATIENT  Stakeholder: COO, Dept Chiefs                                                                                                                                                                 
     FLOW for    First signal: Day 90–180                                                                                                                                                                      
     inpatient / Metrics: encounter volume, panel growth, patients/provider/shift,                                                                                                                             
     THROUGHPUT     LOS indicators, LWBS rate (ED)                                                                                                                                                             
     for ED)                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                            
  THREE PHASES OF VALUE (based on months on Abridge)                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    PHASE 1 — Documentation Fidelity     (Days 0–90)                                                                                                                                                           
    → Quality domain is primary. Efficiency beginning.                                                                                                                                                         
    → Revenue and Workforce: baseline only. No signal expected yet.                                                                                                                                            
    → Capacity/Flow/Throughput: pre-signal. No data to show.                                                                                                                                                   
                                                            
    PHASE 2 — Efficiency                  (Days 90–180)                                                                                                                                                        
    → Workforce signals emerging. Revenue signals strengthening.                                                                                                                                               
    → Quality: first CDI and coding accuracy data.                                                                                                                                                             
    → Capacity/Flow/Throughput: still pre-signal.                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    PHASE 3 — Capacity / Flow / Throughput (Days 180–360)                                                                                                                                                      
    → All four domains now active.                                                                                                                                                                             
    → Capacity (outpatient): encounter volume, panel growth, new settings                                                                                                                                      
    → Patient Flow (inpatient): discharge pipeline, LOS direction                                                                                                                                              
    → Patient Throughput (ED): LWBS rate, patients/provider/shift                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    PHASE 4 — Strategic Proof             (Day 360+)                                                                                                                                                           
    → Multi-domain validated data. Board-level story.
    → Renewal is assumed. Question is where to grow next.                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  FOUR MATURITY STAGES (per partner engagement)
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    UNMEASURED   → No baseline. No story yet.                                                                                                                                                                  
                   MRU stabilizing. Single setting. Relationship-dependent.                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    SIGNALING    → First metrics live. 1–2 domains trending.                                                                                                                                                   
                   MRU growing. Core setting deepening. Trust established.
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    VALIDATED    → Trends confirmed across multiple domains.                                                                                                                                                   
                   Multi-setting. Renewal is a given. QBR-worthy data.                                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
    STRATEGIC    → Embedded in org strategy. Board-level proof.                                                                                                                                                
                   Full portfolio. Expansion is the conversation.                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  MATURITY STAGE DERIVATION (computed, not user-selected)                                                                                                                                                      
    - UNMEASURED: monthsOnAbridge < 3 OR utilizationRate < 20                                                                                                                                                  
    - SIGNALING: monthsOnAbridge 3–9 AND utilizationRate 20–60 AND 1–2 domains have data                                                                                                                       
    - VALIDATED: monthsOnAbridge 9–18 OR (utilizationRate > 60 AND 3+ domains have data)                                                                                                                       
    - STRATEGIC: monthsOnAbridge > 18 AND utilizationRate > 70
