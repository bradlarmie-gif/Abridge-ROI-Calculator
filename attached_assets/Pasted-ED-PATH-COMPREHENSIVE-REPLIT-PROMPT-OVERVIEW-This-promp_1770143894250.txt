Math:
CopyHours Saved = Time Saved (min) × Eligible Encounters ÷ 60

Example with 50 providers, 1,800 enc/prov, 70% util, 2 min saved:
- Eligible Encounters = 50 × 1,800 × 70% = 63,000
- Hours Saved = 2 × 63,000 ÷ 60 = 2,100 hours

LWBS Math:
CopyAnnual ED Volume = Eligible Encounters ÷ Utilization Rate
LWBS Patients = Annual ED Volume × LWBS Rate
Patients Recovered = LWBS Patients × LWBS Reduction %
Gross Value = Patients Recovered × Revenue per Visit
Net Value = Gross Value × Realization Rate

Defaults:
- LWBS Rate: 3%
- LWBS Reduction: 10% / 20% / 30%
- Revenue per Visit: $450
- Realization Rate: 75%
