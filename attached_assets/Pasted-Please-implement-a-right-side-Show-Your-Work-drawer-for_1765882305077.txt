lever **Clinician Retention**, using the exact same visual layout, components,
and behavior as the existing **Patient Access** drawer.

3. ASSUMPTIONS (Editable Inputs)
────────────────────────────────────────

Use the same control styles as the other drawers: sliders where ranges
matter, numeric inputs where precision matters. Group them with a bold
section title: **ASSUMPTIONS**.

(A) Provider population & baseline turnover

1. **Clinicians included in this analysis**
   • Numeric input (integer)
   • Default: pre-filled from “Providers in Scope”
   • Label: “Clinicians included in this analysis”
   • Helper: “Total clinicians in scope for this scenario.”

2. **Baseline annual attrition rate (%)**
   • Slider or numeric (%) input
   • Range: 0–30%
   • Sensible default (e.g., 8–10%)
   • Label: “Baseline annual attrition rate (%)”
   • Helper: “Typical percentage of clinicians leaving per year today.”

3. **% of attrition attributed to burnout / documentation**
   • Slider (%)
   • Range: 0–80%
   • Default: 40%
   • Label: “% of attrition tied to burnout and documentation burden”
   • Helper: “Share of exits where workload and documentation are major drivers.”

(B) Impact of Abridge on burnout-related exits

4. **Expected reduction in burnout-related exits with Abridge (%)**
   • Slider (%)
   • Range: 0–75%
   • Default: 50%
   • Label: “Expected reduction in burnout-related exits with Abridge”
   • Helper: “Portion of burnout-linked departures you expect to prevent.”

5. **Realization factor (% of avoided exits you are comfortable claiming)**
   • Slider (%)
   • Range: 0–100%
   • Default: 75%
   • Label: “Realization factor for avoided exits”
   • Helper: “Conservative share of modeled exits avoided that you are comfortable taking credit for.”

(C) Financial impact per departure

6. **Cost per departure ($)**
   • Numeric input
   • Label: “Cost per departure ($)”
   • Helper: “All-in cost to replace one clinician (recruiting, onboarding, ramp-up, lost productivity).”

────────────────────────────────────────
5. Calculation Logic (Wire into the ROI Engine)
────────────────────────────────────────

Let:

  cliniciansInScope = input (Clinicians included in this analysis)
  attritionRate = baseline annual attrition rate (0–1)
  burnoutShare = % attrition tied to burnout (0–1)
  reductionWithAbridge = expected reduction in burnout exits (0–1)
  realizationFactor = realization factor for avoided exits (0–1)
  costPerDeparture = cost per departure ($)

Compute:

1) totalExits =
      cliniciansInScope * attritionRate

2) burnoutExits =
      totalExits * burnoutShare

3) modeledExitsAvoided =
      burnoutExits * reductionWithAbridge

4) realizedExitsAvoided =
      modeledExitsAvoided * realizationFactor

5) annualSavings =
      realizedExitsAvoided * costPerDeparture

Bind `annualSavings` as the **Clinician Retention** lever’s annual value:
  • Update the Clinician Retention row in “Understanding Your Drivers”
    (Annual Value column).
  • Feed into “Total Annual Impact” and “Net Gain”.
  • Feed into the ROI Waterfall bar for Clinician Retention.
  • Scale proportionally in the Enterprise Expansion View for this lever.
