PAGE 4: VALUE DRIVERS - BRUTAL HONESTY
🎯 What's Working:

✅ The accordion/expandable model - Clean, progressive disclosure
✅ "The Theory" sections - LOVE this. Explains the "why" before the "what"
✅ Step-by-step math - Makes complex ROI feel transparent and defensible
✅ Live calculation - Numbers update as you type, feels dynamic
✅ The benchmark callouts - "Your input (blended)" in red creates urgency to adjust
✅ Final summary box - Green box with the total is satisfying

🚨 What Needs Work:

TOO MUCH COGNITIVE LOAD - Each driver has 5 steps. By the 3rd driver, users are exhausted
The "Your Calculation" header is redundant - We know it's our calculation, just show the theory
Input fields look cramped - Need more breathing room, larger touch targets
The × and = symbols - They're tiny, hard to parse quickly
Benchmark section at bottom - Gets lost. Should be inline or a tooltip
Missing: "Why should I trust this?" - No indication these are industry-validated assumptions


MY REDESIGN APPROACH
Philosophy: Make it feel like Stripe Billing configurator meets Apple product page
Key Changes:

Larger, clearer inputs with better visual hierarchy
Inline benchmarks as context, not separate sections
Collapsible "deep dive" - Simple mode by default, expert mode available
Progress indicator showing which drivers you've reviewed
Sticky summary sidebar showing running total
