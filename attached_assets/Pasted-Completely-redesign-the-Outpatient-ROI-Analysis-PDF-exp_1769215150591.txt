Completely redesign the Outpatient ROI Analysis PDF export. This is the most important export in the entire application — it's what helps close new business. It needs to be PREMIUM, EDUCATIONAL, DENSE, and feel like nothing else in healthcare.

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Think: Stripe's annual reports meets McKinsey's insights meets Apple's confidence.

- **Premium**: Every pixel intentional. Beautiful typography. Sophisticated color use.
- **Educational**: Teach them HOW ambient AI creates value, not just show numbers
- **Dense**: No wasted space. Every page should be rich with valuable content.
- **Personalized**: Their numbers, their drivers, their organization
- **Defensible**: Every number traceable, every assumption visible
