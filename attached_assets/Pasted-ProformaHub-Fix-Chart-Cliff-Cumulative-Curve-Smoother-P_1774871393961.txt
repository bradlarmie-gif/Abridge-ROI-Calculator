ProformaHub — Fix Chart Cliff: Cumulative Curve + Smoother Provider Ramp                                                                                                                                     
                                                                          
  Two changes to fix the near-vertical cliff in the Value Growth Trajectory chart. The cliff is caused by (1) a 3-month provider ramp that's too abrupt and (2) the chart showing per-quarter values which     
  makes onset delays and scale-ups look like step functions. The fix extends the ramp window and switches to a cumulative chart that naturally shows smooth compounding growth.

  File 1: client/src/pages/proforma/proformaTypes.ts                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                          
  Find:                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  implementationRampMonths: 3,                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Replace with:
  implementationRampMonths: 6,                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                          
  This spreads each provider cohort transition (Y1→Y2, Y2→Y3) over 6 months instead of 3, splitting what was a single cliff quarter into a gradual ramp across two quarters. Also more defensible operationally
   — it takes an organization 6 months to onboard a new provider cohort.
