CALCULATIONS:

The gap calculation:

1. Their current state:
   - Documented encounters = Providers × 2000 × Their_utilization
   - Time returned = Documented × Their_time_savings
   - For each driver, calculate value with their metrics

2. Abridge potential:
   - Documented encounters = Providers × 2000 × 0.65 (Abridge avg)
   - Time returned = Documented × 2.5 (Abridge avg)
   - For each driver, calculate value with Abridge metrics

3. Gap = Abridge value - Their value

4. Cost of waiting:
   - Monthly gap = Annual gap ÷ 12
   - 3-year projection = Annual gap × 3
   - Delay cost = Months waited × Monthly gap
