  The outpatient story lives or dies on one insight: scale is the mechanism. 3 minutes × 20 patients × 240 days = 240 hours per provider. That's what the CAPACITY domain leads with — and everything else (the
   burnout, the wRVU, the HCC) is downstream of that same fundamental dynamic.
