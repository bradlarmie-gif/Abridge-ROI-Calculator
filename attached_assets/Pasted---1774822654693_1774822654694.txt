  New mechanism card 1 — Bundle Protocol Compliance                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Title: "Bundle Protocol Compliance (CAUTI, CLABSI, SEP-1)"
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Inside the card:                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Mechanism paragraph:
  Bundle protocols require complete, timely documentation of every step — catheter care, line access, assessment timing. When documentation happens retrospectively during end-of-shift charting, protocol gaps
   are invisible until the event has already occurred. Real-time ambient documentation captures bundle steps at the point of care, making gaps visible while there's still time to intervene.                  
                                                                                                                                                                                             
  Formula block (use the existing bg-[#F5F0EB] rounded-lg p-4 style):
  Current HAI events × documentation-preventable rate × cost per event = potential value                                                                                                                       
  CMS penalty exposure × compliance improvement rate = penalty avoidance
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Key assumptions list:                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - CAUTI cost per event: $13,000–$30,000 (CMS/AHRQ benchmark)                                                                                                                                                 
  - CLABSI cost per event: $46,000–$68,000 (CDC benchmark)    
  - SEP-1 non-compliance: VBC contract-dependent; penalty exposure varies by payer                                                                                                                             
  - Documentation-preventable rate: 5–12% — conservative; represents cases where real-time documentation would have surfaced a protocol gap in time to intervene                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Add a note paragraph:                                                                                                                                                                                      
  Bundle compliance has two distinct financial levers — avoided cost of the event itself, and avoided CMS quality penalties. If your organization participates in value-based care contracts with quality      
  performance components, SEP-1 and bundle compliance rates directly affect contract performance. These numbers already exist in your quality reports.                                                         
                                                                                                                                                      
  "Why this is defensible" callout (orange left border):                                                                                                                                                       
  HAI rates, bundle compliance rates, and CMS penalty exposure are tracked in your quality department today. The before/after comparison is clean. Documentation is the visibility infrastructure — clinical   
  team response is the intervention mechanism. We don't claim the outcome; we claim the foundation it runs on.                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  New mechanism card 2 — Fall Risk Real-Time Assessment                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Title: "Fall Risk — Real-Time Assessment"                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Inside the card:                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  Mechanism paragraph:
  Fall risk assessments are typically documented at shift start and updated retrospectively. With ambient documentation, fall risk factors — patient confusion, medication changes, mobility status — are 
  captured as the clinical picture evolves in real time, not hours later. This timeliness changes whether a fall risk flag reaches the team while the patient is still at risk.
                                          
  Formula block:
  Current fall events × documentation-preventable rate × cost per event = potential value                                                                                                                      
     
  Key assumptions list:                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Average cost per inpatient fall: $14,000–$30,000 (CMS Never Event / litigation exposure)                                                                                                                 
  - Documentation-preventable rate: 5% — conservative; captures cases where a real-time assessment update would have triggered a precaution change before the event

  Add a note paragraph distinguishing this from HAPI:                                                                                                                                                          
  This is modeled separately from HAPI/pressure injury because the documentation timing mechanism is different. Fall risk is dynamic throughout a shift and changes with patient status. Pressure injury risk 
  is assessed less frequently. Real-time capture matters more for fall risk because the window to intervene is shorter.
