      </Text>

      </Text>

        <Text style={styles.bold}>The implication: </Text>

    </View>
  </View>

      </Text>
      
      <Text style={styles.conceptText}>
        <Text style={styles.bold}>Management overhead: </Text>
        Scribe programs require supervision, scheduling, quality review, and HR management. This overhead typically adds 10-15% to direct costs but is often absorbed into existing staff time (and not tracked).
      </Text>
      
      <Text style={styles.conceptText}>
        <Text style={styles.bold}>Coverage gaps: </Text>
        PTO, sick days, and turnover create coverage gaps. When a scribe is out, the provider goes back to self-documenting — the burden returns unpredictably.
      </Text>

  </View>
