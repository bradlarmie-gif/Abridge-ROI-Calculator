Summary of Changes

Start with a reminder - "Here's what you compared"
Scale controls feel impactful - Show the multiplier effect clearly
Driver cards show the actual difference - Not just $ values, but the real metrics (visits, hours, etc.)
Visual comparison is obvious - Before → After, with clear gap bar
Chart tells the compounding story - Year-by-year breakdown
Bottom line punch - Big dramatic number on dark background

The story now flows: Baseline → Scale Up → See Where It Comes From → Watch It Compound → Feel the Weight of the Opportunity
