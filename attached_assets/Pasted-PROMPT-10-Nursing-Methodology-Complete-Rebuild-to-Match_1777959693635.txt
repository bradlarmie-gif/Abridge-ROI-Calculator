  Nursing is structurally different from the physician settings:                                                                                                                                               
  - Nurses are hourly, not salaried — documentation time that overruns the shift = overtime. This is the only care setting where time savings convert directly to a payroll dollar.
  - Nurses don't generate billing codes — so the REVENUE domain tells a different story: HCAHPS/VBP performance and HAC penalty avoidance are the CMS programs where nursing documentation quality touches real
   Medicare reimbursement.                                                                                                                                                                                     
  - The workflow is point-of-care batching vs. real-time capture — the defining behavioral shift ambient nursing enables.
