# Risk Domain — Final Fixes

These are targeted changes to the current Risk domain implementation. The level structure is correct. These are polish fixes to make it airtight.

---

## Level 1 — Better Notes, Same Infrastructure
**No changes needed.** This is clean. No inputs, output text is strong.

---

## Level 2 — Active Quality Monitoring

### Fix 1: Don't pre-select the radio option
The "Not yet" option should NOT be pre-selected when the user arrives at Level 2. All three radio options should be unselected. The estimated impact card should show:

```
ESTIMATED IMPACT
—

Select how your organization is monitoring
documentation quality to see your assessment.
```

### Fix 2: Handle the "Not yet" selection gracefully
If someone selects Level 2 but then picks "Not yet" in the radio:

```
ESTIMATED IMPACT
—

Documentation quality monitoring hasn't started yet.
If no one is reviewing documentation attributes —
even informally — your organization may be at Level 1
for this domain.

The single most important next step is establishing
any form of documentation quality review.

Organizations that begin systematic monitoring have
reported 15-30% improvement in documentation
completeness and specificity. Based on aggregated
deployment experience.
```

This gently suggests they might be Level 1 without forcing them to change. They can stay at Level 2 if they want — it's their self-assessment.

### Fix 3: "Spot checks" and "Systematic tracking" outputs

**If "Spot checks":**
```
ESTIMATED IMPACT
Informal monitoring active

Your organization is informally reviewing documentation
quality through spot checks and anecdotal feedback.
This is a meaningful step — but informal processes
don't scale and can't drive organizational strategy.

Consider formalizing a review cadence and measurement
framework to move toward Level 3.

Organizations with formalized monitoring have reported
measurable improvements in documentation completeness,
coding specificity, and audit readiness. Based on
aggregated deployment experience.
```

**If "Systematic tracking":**
Show the checkbox input (what attributes are you tracking). Current implementation looks correct — keep the 5 checkboxes. Output should show:

```
ESTIMATED IMPACT
[checkedCount] of 5 quality dimensions tracked

Your organization is systematically tracking
[checkedCount] documentation quality attribute(s):
[list of checked items]

Not yet tracked:
[list of unchecked items]

Organizations tracking 4+ attributes are well-positioned
to connect documentation quality to downstream workflows
(Level 3).

Estimates based on your inputs. Individual results vary.
```

## Level 3 — Downstream Systems Connected

### Fix 1: Make hours input optional
The checkboxes should work independently. The hours input is supplementary.

Reframe the hours input label:
- Old: "Estimated total hours saved per month across connected workflows"
- **New:** "If known: estimated hours saved per month across these workflows"
- Add helper text: *"Optional. If you can estimate the combined time savings across the workflows you selected, enter it here. If not, the workflow connections above are the primary assessment."*

### Fix 2: Output without hours entered

If checkboxes selected but no hours:
```
ESTIMATED IMPACT
[checkedCount] downstream workflow(s) connected

Your documentation infrastructure is driving
improvement across [checkedCount] downstream
workflow(s):
[list of checked items]

Not yet connected:
[list of unchecked items]

The workflows you haven't connected represent
untapped operational value from your documentation
foundation.

Estimates based on your inputs. Individual results vary.
```

If checkboxes AND hours:
```
ESTIMATED IMPACT
$[annualSavings] in operational value

Your documentation infrastructure is driving
improvement across [checkedCount] downstream
workflow(s):
[list of checked items]

[annualHours] hours recaptured annually — valued
at $[annualSavings].

[annualHours] = [hoursSaved] × 12
[annualSavings] = [annualHours] × $75/hr
Rate: $75/hr blended abstraction/administrative rate.

Not yet connected:
[list of unchecked items]

Estimates based on your inputs. Individual results vary.
```

## Level 4 — Documentation as Strategic Data Asset

### Fix 1: Soften the malpractice checkbox
- Old: "Risk management / malpractice review (documentation defensibility is tracked)"
- **New:** "Clinical documentation review (documentation completeness tracked as part of risk oversight)"

This keeps it about documentation completeness as an organizational practice without claiming malpractice protection.

### Fix 2: Update all benchmark text to legal-safe language
- Old: "Abridge enterprise customers at this level report $100K-$500K+ in attributed strategic value"
- **New:** "Organizations at the highest maturity level have reported $100K-$500K+ in attributed strategic value. Based on aggregated deployment experience."

## Legal Language — Apply Across All Risk Levels

Update ALL benchmark text in the Risk domain to remove "Abridge customers" and replace with "Organizations" + "Based on aggregated deployment experience."

Specifically:
- Level 2 benchmark: "Organizations that begin systematic monitoring have reported 15-30% improvement..."
- Level 3 benchmark: "Organizations with connected downstream workflows have reported 10-40 hrs/month in combined efficiency gains. Based on aggregated deployment experience."
- Level 4 benchmark: "Organizations at the highest maturity level have reported $100K-$500K+ in attributed strategic value. Based on aggregated deployment experience."
