STEP 1: TIME RETURNED
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Minutes saved × Documented encounters = Hours back │
│ 2.5 min × 52,000 = 2,167 hours                     │

STEP 2: REALITY CHECK
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Not all time converts to visits                     │
│ Realization factor: 20% (typical)                   │
│ 2,167 hrs × 20% = 433 usable hours                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

STEP 3: NEW VISITS
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Usable hours ÷ Visit duration = New visits         │
│ 433 hrs ÷ 30 min = 866 additional visits           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

STEP 4: REVENUE IMPACT
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ New visits × Revenue per visit = Annual value      │
│ 866 × $200 = $173,200                              │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
