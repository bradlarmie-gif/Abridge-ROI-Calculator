# INPATIENT — THE REAL STORY

## The Industry Question:

*"What is Abridge going to do in the inpatient setting... and is it a big deal or not?"*

## The Honest Answer:

**For hospitalists, the value story is different than outpatient.**

In outpatient:
- Time savings → More patients → Revenue
- wRVU lift → Revenue

In inpatient:
- Time savings → Better work-life, maybe handle larger census
- DRG accuracy → Revenue (this is the big one)
- CDI efficiency → Operational savings

**The hero isn't time. The hero is documentation quality driving revenue integrity.**

## Page 2: Time Savings

**Reframe for hospitalists:**

```
TIME SAVINGS

How much time could your hospitalists get back?

Hospitalists document across the patient stay—H&Ps, progress notes, 
discharge summaries. Abridge reduces documentation time across all of these.

WHAT THE DATA SHOWS

Across inpatient implementations, hospitalists typically save 15-45 minutes 
per admission on total documentation time.

Source: Abridge customer data, 2024-2025

CHOOSE YOUR SCENARIO

○ Conservative    15 min
  Per admission (combined across all notes)

● Typical         30 min      RECOMMENDED
  Based on average outcomes across similar implementations

○ Optimistic      45 min
  For high-adoption programs with strong workflows
```

**Math:**
```
Hours Saved = Time per Admission × Eligible Admissions ÷ 60

Example: 30 min × 84,000 admissions ÷ 60 = 42,000 hours/year
```
