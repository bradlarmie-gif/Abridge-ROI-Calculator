FIX THE SCRIBE PATH - SPLIT THE CRAMPED SCREEN INTO SEPARATE PAGES

## THE PROBLEM
The current "cost comparison" screen has too many elements:
- Cost comparison cards
- "See how Abridge creates value" link
- "See hidden costs" button  
- "What matters most" section
- Driver selection cards
- Estimated gap
- Continue button

This needs to be 3 SEPARATE screens.

## THE FIX

### SCREEN 4: Cost Comparison
This screen should ONLY contain:
1. Title: "The cost comparison"
2. Subtitle: "Scribes vs. Abridge"
3. Two side-by-side cards:
   - Left: Your Scribe Program (cost, providers covered, math)
   - Right: With Abridge (pricing note, all providers get access, no per-hour)
4. One CTA button: "See hidden costs →"

REMOVE from this screen:
- "See how Abridge creates value beyond cost" link
- "What matters most to your organization" section
- All driver selection cards
- Estimated Annual Gap box
- The cramped footer

### SCREEN 5: Hidden Costs
This screen should ONLY contain:
1. Title: "The costs you don't see"
2. Subtitle: "Scribes come with operational overhead"
3. Three info cards (Turnover, Management, Coverage Gaps)
4. Total Hidden Costs summary
5. One CTA button: "See what else Abridge adds →"

### SCREEN 6: Value Drivers (NEW SEPARATE SCREEN)
This screen should contain:
1. Title: "What scribes can't do"
2. Subtitle: "Abridge adds intelligence, not just documentation"
3. Intro text: "Select what matters to your organization:"
4. Driver selection cards (the ones currently crammed onto cost comparison)
5. "Show 3 more drivers" expand
6. Running total at bottom: "Selected: X of 6 | Estimated additional value: $XX/year"
7. TWO CTA options:
   - Secondary: "Skip - just show cost savings" (goes to summary without drivers)
   - Primary: "Calculate value →" (goes to summary with drivers)

### SCREEN 7: Summary
Combines everything into final view.
