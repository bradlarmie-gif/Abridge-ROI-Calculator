Visual Hierarchy Fixes
1. The "65,000 eligible encounters" output is buried
This is THE number everything builds from. Right now it's a small line at the bottom of the Organization section.
Make it a prominent callout:
Copy→ 65,000 eligible encounters
  This is your multiplier. Everything below builds from this.
Styled as a highlighted box or with larger text and an arrow/icon indicating "this flows down."
2. Result numbers should be green
The dollar amounts ($216,667, $81,900, $52,650) are positive outcomes. Style them in green to reinforce "this is value you're gaining." The coral/orange should be reserved for the NET GAIN summary and CTAs.
3. The Live Model sidebar is doing too much
It's showing inputs AND value breakdown AND net gain. Simplify to just the essentials:

Remove the itemized breakdown from the sidebar—that detail lives in the expanded driver cards on the left. The sidebar is just the scoreboard.
4. One driver expanded at a time
When "Patient Access" is expanded, "Accurate Level of Service" and "Clinician Retention" should be collapsed. This focuses the conversation and reduces visual overwhelm. Accordion behavior.
5. The Investment section needs separation
It currently runs right into the value drivers. Add a clear visual break—a horizontal rule or distinct card—and consider a header like:
CopyYour Investment
What does this cost?
6. Button treatments in driver cards
The "Yes, waitlists / Probably yes / Not sure" and "Conservative 15% / Typical 20% / Optimistic 30%" buttons are good but need cleaner styling:

Selected state should be clearly filled (not just outlined)
Unselected should be subtle/ghost style
Equal widths for the button group


Micro-interactions
1. Numbers should animate
When inputs change and the Result updates, the number should ease/count to the new value (200-300ms). This creates the feeling that the model is alive and responsive.
2. Smooth accordion transitions
When expanding/collapsing driver cards, the height transition should be smooth (250ms ease), not instant.
