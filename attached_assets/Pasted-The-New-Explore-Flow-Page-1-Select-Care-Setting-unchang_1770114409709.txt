The Math — What's Actually Defensible
Time Savings (Provable)
CopyHours Saved = Time Saved per Encounter × Eligible Encounters ÷ 60

Where:
- Time Saved: 2 / 4 / 6 min (user choice)
- Eligible Encounters = Total Encounters × Utilization Rate
wRVU Improvement (Provable with caveats)
CopyAdditional wRVUs = Eligible Encounters × wRVU Lift × Realization Rate
Revenue = Additional wRVUs × $33

Where:
- wRVU Lift: 2% / 5% / 7% (user choice)
- Realization Rate: 75% (conservative default)
- $33 = Medicare conversion factor
HCC Capture (Provable if MA population)
CopyHCC Value = MA Patients × Gap Rate × Recapture Target × Avg HCCs × RAF Impact × Annual Payment × Realization

Where:
- MA Patients = Providers × Panel Size × MA %
- Gap Rate: 40% (industry average)
- Recapture Target: 10% / 15% / 25% (user choice)
- Avg HCCs per gap patient: 1.5
- RAF Impact: 0.4
- Annual Payment per RAF: $12,000
- Realization: 60% (RADV audits, etc.)
Denial Prevention (Provable if they have denial data)
CopyDenial Value = Eligible Encounters × Denial Rate × Unappealable % × Prevention Target × Avg Claim Value × Realization

Where:
- Denial Rate: 8% (industry average, editable)
- Unappealable %: 40%
- Prevention Target: 25% / 50% / 75% (user choice)
- Avg Claim Value: $250 (editable)
- Realization: 85%
Time Value — NOT PROVABLE, BUT FRAMEABLE
Instead of calculating "Patient Access = $X," we say:
"If your organization used reclaimed time for additional patient visits:"

User inputs: % of time toward capacity, visit duration, revenue per visit
We calculate: Potential visits × Revenue per visit
We caveat: "This assumes schedulable time and available demand"

"If your organization saw direct cost reductions:"

User inputs: Estimated annual savings (direct input)
We display: Their number
We caveat: "Based on your estimate"

"If this time improved clinician wellbeing:"

We calculate: Hours/week back per provider
We don't monetize it
We note: "1 provider retained = $300-500K in replacement costs"
