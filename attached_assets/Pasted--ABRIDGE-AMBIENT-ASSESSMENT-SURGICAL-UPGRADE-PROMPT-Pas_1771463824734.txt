## CONTEXT: WHAT THIS IS AND WHAT WE'RE DOING

This is not a rebuild. This is a sequence redesign and UX elevation of the existing Abridge Ambient Assessment.

The existing tool quantifies well. But it doesn't disorient first. It flows when it should jolt. It concludes when it should haunt. We are fixing that.

Keep: All existing calculation logic, branding (EA2C00 red, Manrope font, Abridge logo), the four value engine screens, uncertainty calibration logic, and the enterprise synthesis output.

Change: The sequence, the emotional architecture of each screen, the copy tone, and the UX layout principles.

The person taking this assessment currently uses a competing ambient solution. They believe they've solved their documentation problem. By the end they should feel they've been thinking embarrassingly small — and be hungry to understand what they're missing.

**Hungry people act. That is the only goal.**

---

## GLOBAL UX PRINCIPLES — APPLY TO EVERY SCREEN

These are non-negotiable. Every screen gets reviewed against these before it ships.

**1. One idea per screen. Never two.**
If a screen is trying to say two things, it's saying nothing. Cut or split.

**2. White space is not empty space. It is the work.**
Content should breathe like a premium medical journal, not a SaaS dashboard. If it feels too sparse, you're probably right. Resist adding.

**3. Cognitive load is the enemy.**
Every element on screen must justify its existence. Decorative elements: zero. Redundant labels: zero. If someone has to think about the UI, the UI has failed.

**4. Numbers are verdicts. Treat them that way.**
Big numbers get their own space. They are centered, large, and surrounded by silence. Never buried in a card grid without breathing room first.

**5. Loss framing over gain framing — always.**
"You are currently losing X" lands 2x harder than "You could gain X." Rewrite every engine screen to lead with what is currently being lost, not what could be captured.

**6. CTAs are directional, not generic.**
No screen ends with "Next →". Every CTA tells them exactly where they're going and creates forward momentum. Examples: "Show Me My Gap →" / "See What This Costs You →" / "Build My Value Map →"

**7. Mobile-first. No exceptions.**
CMOs open things on their phones at HIMSS. Every screen must be flawless at 390px width. Pill selectors stack. Cards go full width. Numbers stay large.

**8. Transitions: fade, 300ms, purposeful.**
Not bouncy. Not sliding carousels. A clean fade between screens signals that something important just ended and something important is beginning.

### SCREEN 0: ENTRY GATE (NEW)
**Purpose:** In 10 seconds, the user self-identifies as someone who doesn't actually know what documentation costs them. They indict themselves. You never have to say it.

**Layout:** Full screen. Vertically and horizontally centered. Nothing else on the page except the Abridge logo top-left and no progress dots (this is the foyer).

**Headline (display, large, bold, centered):**
> "What is documentation actually costing your organization annually?"

**Three pill options, centered, generous padding:**
- "I have a number"
- "I have a rough estimate"
- "I've never calculated it"

**Behavior:** Selecting any option immediately shows a one-line response (fade in below the pills, 400ms delay):
- "I have a number" → *"Let's validate that number."*
- "I have a rough estimate" → *"Let's sharpen it."*
- "I've never calculated it" → *"Most don't. That's exactly why this exists."*

Then auto-advance to Screen 1 after 1.8 seconds. No button needed.

**Design note:** The third response — "Most don't" — should land with zero judgment. It's a relief, not an indictment. The tone is a calm, knowing colleague. Not a salesperson.

## COPY TONE RULES — APPLY EVERYWHERE

- **No exclamation points. Ever.**
- **No "unlock," "transform," "revolutionize," or "empower."** These are the words of vendors. This tool speaks like a strategist.
- **Short sentences.** If a sentence exceeds 18 words, cut it.
- **"Already embedded in your operations"** — use this phrase or close variations throughout Screens 6–12. It turns gain into recovery. Recovery is more urgent than gain.
- **Statements over questions** — except Screen 0 (Entry) and Screen 13 (Exit). Questions at those two moments are intentional and powerful. Everywhere else, statements signal authority.
- **Numbers get their own line.** Never bury a dollar figure mid-sentence mid-paragraph.
