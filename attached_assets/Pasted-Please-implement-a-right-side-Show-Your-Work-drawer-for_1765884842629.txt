────────────────────────────────────────
5. Calculation Logic (Bind to ROI Engine)
────────────────────────────────────────

Let:

  patients = risk-based patients in this analysis
  avgConditions = avg chronic conditions per patient
  missedPct = % present but undocumented (0–1)
  capturePct = % of missed conditions captured with Abridge (0–1)
  newlyIdentifiedPct = % of newly surfaced conditions (0–1)
  realization = realization factor (0–1)
  revenuePerCondition = incremental revenue per condition ($)

Compute:

1) totalConditions =
      patients * avgConditions

2) missedConditions =
      totalConditions * missedPct

3) capturedMissedConditions =
      missedConditions * capturePct

4) newlyIdentifiedConditions =
      totalConditions * newlyIdentifiedPct

5) modeledCaptured =
      capturedMissedConditions + newlyIdentifiedConditions

6) realizedCaptured =
      modeledCaptured * realization

7) annualImpact =
      realizedCaptured * revenuePerCondition

Bind:
• annualImpact → HCC & Chronic Condition Capture lever’s Annual Value
• Feed into Total Annual Impact, Net Gain, Return (x)
• Update waterfall bar for “HCC”
• Update Enterprise Expansion View scaled by enterprise encounters

────────────────────────────────────────
6. Formula Block — “How this is calculated”
────────────────────────────────────────

At the bottom, add muted header:
  **HOW THIS IS CALCULATED**

Monospace block:

"Annual revenue impact =
  Risk-based patients
  × Average chronic conditions per patient
  × % of present conditions not documented
  × % captured with Abridge
  + (Risk-based patients × Average conditions × % newly identified)
  → multiplied by realization factor
  → multiplied by incremental revenue per condition"
