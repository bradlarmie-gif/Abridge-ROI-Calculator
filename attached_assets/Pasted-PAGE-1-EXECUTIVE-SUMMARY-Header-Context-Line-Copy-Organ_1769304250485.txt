WHAT THE MULTIPLIERS MEAN
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Provider scaling (3.0x)
Linear multiplication of your current per-provider value. This is 
straightforward math: 3× the providers, 3× the documented encounters, 
3× the value.

Utilization improvement (1.17x)
You're currently at 70% utilization. We assume you'll reach 82% at 
scale—consistent with what mature deployments achieve. Higher 
utilization means more encounters documented per provider, which 
increases value proportionally.
