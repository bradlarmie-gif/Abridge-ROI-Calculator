GLOBAL TONE PRINCIPLES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Replace "value left on the table" with "potential value" or "opportunity"
2. Replace "every month you wait" with "monthly opportunity" 
3. Replace "the right partnership" with specific benefits or softer language
4. Replace alarming warnings (⚠️ ALL CAPS) with observational language
5. Add phrases like "worth understanding," "worth discussing," "if significant"
6. Acknowledge their current decision was reasonable, the gap is common
7. Position Abridge as helpful resource, not aggressive competitor
8. Let the math speak—don't add emotional pressure on top of it
