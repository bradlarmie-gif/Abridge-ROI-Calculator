  ▎                                                         
  ▎ Abridge internal teams use Forecast to answer one question for a partner: "Based on the results you've measured so far, and the dollar assumptions we can make about them, how does your ROI change under  
  ▎ different pricing structures, adoption speeds, and utilization assumptions?"
  ▎                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  ▎ So the dashboard's story arc is:                        
  ▎ 1. Here are your measured value drivers (from Measure, or manually entered — quantified in dollars)
  ▎ 2. Here's your ROI under your current contract                                                                                                                                                             
  ▎ 3. Here's how that changes under alternate pricing models ← the killer moment                                                                                                                              
  ▎ 4. Pull these levers (adoption, utilization, growth, encounter share) to see what moves                                                                                                                    
  ▎                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  ▎ Right now the dashboard loads blank (no value drivers → $0 TCV → -$3.3M net). That's the biggest symptom. Value drivers need to be present by default, editable, prominent — not a collapsible accordion   
  ▎ the user has to discover.
