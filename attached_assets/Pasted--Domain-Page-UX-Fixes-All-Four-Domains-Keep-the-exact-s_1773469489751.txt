# Domain Page UX Fixes — All Four Domains

Keep the exact same visual design, colors, card treatments, typography, and layout. These are behavioral and content fixes to how the levels display and interact. No new visual patterns.

---

## Fix 1: Previous levels should never show "No stage selected" or "—"

When a user clicks Level 3, Levels 1 and 2 appear above it with check marks. Currently, if the user didn't fill in those levels' inputs, the status shows "No stage selected" or "—" which looks broken.

**New behavior:** When a user selects any level, all levels below it automatically show as passed through. The status text should be:

For levels that have data entered from earlier (e.g., time saved from baseline feeds Level 1):
```
✓ Time Recovered                    1,600 hrs/yr
```
Show the actual value.

For levels that don't have data but are below the selected level:
```
✓ Access Decision Made              Confirmed
```
Show "Confirmed" — not "No stage selected" and not "—". Being at Level 3 implies you've passed through Level 2. The word "Confirmed" communicates that without requiring the user to go back and fill in Level 2's radio buttons.

**Apply this across all four domains.** Every level below the selected level shows a check mark with either its calculated value (if data exists) or "Confirmed" (if no data but the level is implicitly passed through).

---

## Fix 2: Level 4 descriptions should be readable when not selected

Currently when a level is grayed out (not selected), the user sees the level name and a short subtitle. The full description only appears when they click into it.

**New behavior:** Show a slightly longer description on the grayed-out Level 4 card — enough that an executive can read what Level 4 represents without clicking it. This is important because Level 4 is the aspirational ceiling. The executive should always be able to see what "great" looks like even when they're at Level 1.

For each domain, the grayed-out Level 4 card should show:

**Capacity Level 4 (when not selected):**
```
4   Access Impact Tracked
    Track downstream access outcomes — panel growth,
    referral conversion, third-next-available — attributed
    to recovered time.
```

**Revenue Level 4 (when not selected):**
```
4   Documentation as a Revenue Lever
    Documentation intelligence drives revenue strategy,
    payer positioning, and financial planning.
```

**Workforce Level 4 (when not selected):**
```
4   Workforce Strategically Managed
    Documentation burden reduction informs recruitment,
    retention programs, and staffing decisions.
```

**Quality Level 4 (when not selected):**
```
4   Documentation as a Strategic Asset
    Structured documentation informs quality programs,
    value-based care, compliance, and AI readiness.
```

Keep the grayed-out visual treatment. Just show enough text that the executive reads it and thinks "we're not there yet." The gap between where they are and Level 4 should be visible the entire time they're on the domain page.

Apply the same treatment to Level 3 when grayed out — show a one-line description. Levels 1 and 2 can keep their current shorter display since they're the starting points, not the aspirational targets.

## Fix 3: Estimated Impact card should acknowledge the level journey

When someone selects Level 3, the estimated impact card should briefly acknowledge what's implied by being at this level before showing the Level 3 specific content.

**Current behavior:** The estimated impact card only shows Level 3's output. No acknowledgment of Levels 1-2 being passed through.

**New behavior:** Add one line of context at the top of the impact card that connects the journey. Keep it short — one sentence.

**Capacity Level 3 impact card:**
```
ESTIMATED IMPACT

$880K in access revenue annually

Your organization has recovered time and decided to
deploy it toward access. Here's what that's producing:

8 additional patients/provider/month × 50 providers
× 11 clinical months = 4,400 visits × $200 = $880K

[rest of impact card content]
```

**Revenue Level 2 impact card:**
```
ESTIMATED IMPACT

$120K-$360K directional estimate

Your organization has observed documentation affecting
reimbursement. Here's your directional estimate:

[rest of impact card content]
```

**Workforce Level 2 impact card:**
```
ESTIMATED IMPACT

2,875 hours reclaimed annually

Your organization is measuring in-clinic burden
reduction. Here's what the data shows:

[rest of impact card content]
```

**Quality Level 3 impact card:**
```
ESTIMATED IMPACT

3 downstream areas connected

Your organization is actively monitoring documentation
quality and connecting it to downstream workflows:

[rest of impact card content]
```

The opening sentence is always: "Your organization has [what L1-L2 accomplished]. Here's [what this level shows]." One sentence. It connects the dot without adding clutter.

## Fix 5: Domain Summary in sidebar should show the complete picture

The sidebar summary for each domain should show ALL levels' outputs — not just the selected level. This creates the layered story.

**Capacity Summary when Level 3 is selected:**
```
CAPACITY SUMMARY

Time Recovered      1,600 hrs/yr (0.9 FTE)
Access Revenue      $880K
Access Outcomes     —

Total Capacity      $880K
```

Time Recovered shows even though they're at Level 3 — because it was calculated from their baseline inputs. Access Revenue shows because they entered Level 3 data. Access Outcomes shows "—" because they're not at Level 4.

**Revenue Summary when Level 2 is selected:**
```
REVENUE SUMMARY

Signals Observed    4 areas
Directional Est.    $120K-$360K
Measured Impact     —
Strategic Revenue   —
```

Signals and estimate show because they're at Level 2. Measured and strategic show "—" because they haven't gotten there.

**Workforce Summary when Level 2 is selected:**
```
WORKFORCE SUMMARY

After-Hours Returned    4,600 hrs/yr
In-Clinic Returned      2,875 hrs/yr
Survey Findings         3 validated
Retention Exposure      —
Strategic Integrations  —
```

After-Hours shows from Level 1 (pre-filled from baseline). In-Clinic and Survey show from Level 2 data. Retention and Strategic show "—" because they're at Level 2.

**Quality Summary when Level 1 is selected:**
```
QUALITY SUMMARY

Quality Tracking        —
Downstream Connected    —
Strategic Areas         —
```

All dashes because Level 1 has no quantifiable output.

**The key:** The summary shows the full ladder. Completed levels have values. Future levels show "—". The executive can see at a glance what they have and what they don't. The dashes ARE the gap.

**Fix the existing display bug:** The Workforce Summary currently shows "—" for After-Hours Returned even when Level 1 has calculated hours. Make sure each summary line correctly pulls from its corresponding level's calculation.

## Fix 6: Domain intro text should set the right frame

Each domain has an intro paragraph at the top. These should be sharp enough to frame the question before the executive selects their level. Review and update if needed:

**Capacity intro (current is good, keep it):**
Something like: "Ambient reduces documentation time. This section measures how that time is being used."

**Revenue intro — update to remove "revenue cycle" focus:**
Change from revenue-cycle-centric language to: "Improved documentation can affect coding accuracy, denials, and reimbursement. This measures whether your organization is tracking it."

**Workforce intro (current is good, keep it):**
Something like: "Reduced documentation burden affects provider time, satisfaction, and retention. This measures how far those effects have been tracked."

**Quality intro — update to match the rebuild:**
Change to: "Improved notes are the starting point. The real value depends on whether that improvement reaches coding, quality reporting, compliance, and future AI initiatives."
