CALCULATION FORMULAS (for reference)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PATIENT ACCESS:
Value = ((Minutes_saved × Eligible_encounters / 60) × Realization_factor × 60 / Visit_duration) × Revenue_per_visit

ACCURATE LEVEL OF SERVICE:
Value = Eligible_encounters × Baseline_wRVU × Doc_quality_lift × Revenue_per_wRVU

CLINICIAN RETENTION:
Value = Providers × Turnover_rate × 0.40 × Abridge_prevention × Replacement_cost
(Note: 0.40 is fixed burnout attribution - not exposed to user)
(Note: Abridge_prevention comes from posture: 0.30/0.40/0.50)

HCC CAPTURE:
Unique_patients = Eligible_encounters / 2.5
MA_patients = Unique_patients × MA_population_percent
Conditions_missed = MA_patients × 2.5 × 0.30
Conditions_recaptured = Conditions_missed × Recapture_rate
RAF_increase_per_patient = (Conditions_recaptured × 0.25) / MA_patients
Value = MA_patients × (Benchmark_PMPM × 12 × RAF_increase_per_patient)

DENIAL REDUCTION:
Total_revenue = Eligible_encounters × Revenue_per_visit (use Patient Access revenue or $200 default)
Revenue_denied = Total_revenue × Baseline_denial_rate
Doc_denials = Revenue_denied × 0.30
Value = Doc_denials × Prevention_rate

OVERTIME COST AVOIDANCE:
Total_hours = Minutes_saved × Eligible_encounters / 60 (use Patient Access minutes saved)
Premium_hours = Total_hours × After_hours_reduction
Value = Premium_hours × Blended_premium_rate
