## PAGE 2: TIME SAVINGS — NEEDS COMPLETE REFRAME

**Current problem:** "2-6 minutes per encounter" doesn't make sense for nursing.

### The Reality of Nursing Documentation:

Nurses don't have "encounters." They have:
1. **Shift-based documentation** — Assessments, vitals documentation, I&O
2. **Event-based documentation** — Medication administration, interventions
3. **Handoff documentation** — Shift change reports

### Recommended Framing: TIME SAVED PER SHIFT

```
TIME SAVINGS

Nursing documentation happens throughout every shift. Abridge can reduce 
time spent on flowsheets, assessments, and handoff documentation.

WHAT THE DATA SHOWS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Nurses spend 25-35% of their shift on documentation. Across implementations, 
ambient documentation can reduce this burden by 15-30 minutes per shift.

Source: Nursing workflow studies, Abridge customer data


CHOOSE YOUR SCENARIO
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

○  Conservative     15 min/shift
   Minimal adoption, limited flowsheet coverage

●  Typical          20 min/shift       RECOMMENDED
   Standard adoption across key flowsheets

○  Optimistic       30 min/shift
   Full adoption with strong change management


WHAT THIS MEANS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Shifts per year:                    109,500
(300 nurses × 365 days × coverage factor)

Time saved per year:                36,500 hours
(20 min × 109,500 shifts ÷ 60)

Per nurse per week:                 2.3 hours returned
```

### The Math:

```
Annual Shifts = Nurse FTEs × 365 × Shift Coverage Factor (default 1.0)
Hours Saved = Time per Shift × Annual Shifts ÷ 60

Or use Eligible Shifts from Page 1:
Eligible Shifts = Annual Shifts × Utilization Rate
Hours Saved = Time per Shift × Eligible Shifts ÷ 60
```
