NEW CALCULATION LOGIC:

INPUTS TO ADD:
- Average panel size per provider (default: 1,500 patients)
- % patients in Medicare Advantage (keep 30%, editable)
- Documentation gap rate (default: 40% - patients with missed HCCs)
- Avg missed HCCs per gap patient (default: 1.5)
- Recapture target % (keep 15%, editable)
- RAF impact per HCC (default: 0.4, editable)
- Annual payment per RAF point (default: $12,000, editable)
- Realization rate (keep 60%, editable)

CALCULATION STEPS:

1. Total patient panel
   = Providers × Panel size per provider

2. MA patient population  
   = Total patients × MA percentage

3. Patients with documentation gaps
   = MA patients × Gap rate

4. Potential HCCs to recapture
   = Gap patients × Avg missed HCCs per patient

5. HCCs you'll recapture
   = Potential HCCs × Recapture target %

6. Value per HCC
   = RAF impact × Annual payment per RAF point

7. Gross annual value
   = HCCs recaptured × Value per HCC

8. Net annual value  
   = Gross value × Realization rate
