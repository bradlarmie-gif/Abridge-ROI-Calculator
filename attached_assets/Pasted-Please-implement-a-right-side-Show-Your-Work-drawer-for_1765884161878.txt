5. Calculation Logic (Bind to ROI Engine)
────────────────────────────────────────

Let:

  revenue = net collectible outpatient revenue
  denialRate = baseline denial rate (0–1)
  recoveredPct = % of denied revenue typically recovered (0–1)
  unrecoverablePct = % of remaining denied revenue unrecoverable (0–1)
  reductionPct = % reduction in documentation-driven denials with Abridge (0–1)
  realization = realization factor (0–1)

Compute:

1) baselineDeniedRevenue =
      revenue * denialRate

2) unrecoveredAfterRework =
      baselineDeniedRevenue * (1 - recoveredPct)

3) documentationDrivenUnrecoverable =
      unrecoveredAfterRework * unrecoverablePct

4) modeledRecovered =
      documentationDrivenUnrecoverable * reductionPct

5) realizedRecovered =
      modeledRecovered * realization

Bind:

• `realizedRecovered` → lever’s Annual Value  
• Feed into:
   – Total Annual Impact  
   – Net Gain  
   – Return (x)  
   – Waterfall bar (“Denials”)  
   – Enterprise Expansion scaling
