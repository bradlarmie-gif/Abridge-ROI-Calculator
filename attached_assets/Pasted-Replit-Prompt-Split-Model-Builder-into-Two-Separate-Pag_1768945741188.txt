Replit Prompt: Split Model Builder into Two Separate Pages
Context: This is an ROI calculator used during live Zoom sales conversations. The current "Build Your Model" page is trying to do too much—organization inputs, value drivers, AND investment all on one scrolling page. It feels like a form, not a premium experience. We need to split this into two distinct pages with clear purpose, pacing, and visual confidence. Think Apple's product configuration: one step at a time, each step feels intentional.

NEW STRUCTURE: Two Pages
Page 1: "Your Value" (Step 3 of 5)
This page is ONLY about understanding the value. No investment, no cost. Just: here's your organization, here are your drivers, here's what value looks like.
Page 2: "Your Investment" (Step 4 of 5)
This page is ONLY about the cost and the final ROI. Simple inputs, clear math, big reveal.
