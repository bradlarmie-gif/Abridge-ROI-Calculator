CONTEXT
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  You've already implemented OUTPATIENT_METRICS, ED_METRICS, and INPATIENT_METRICS in measureCareSettings.ts. Now do the same for Nursing. Follow the exact same pattern.
                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Key nursing distinctions from the physician settings:     
  - Abridge for nursing reduces documentation burden on flowsheets, assessments, handoff notes, and care plans — not SOAP notes
  - No E/M or DRG coding. Revenue contribution comes through CDI documentation quality and nursing-sensitive billing accuracy                                                                                  
  - The signature nursing quality metrics are NSIs (Nursing-Sensitive Indicators): falls, HAPIs, CLABSI, CAUTI                                                                                                 
  - Workforce is the highest-stakes domain — nursing has the highest turnover rates of any clinical role and the largest workforce cost exposure                                                               
  - The Phase 3 label for nursing is "Staffing" — documentation burden reduction translates to agency/travel nurse reduction and scheduling stability                                                          
  - Nurse turnover cost is $40K–$65K per RN (vs $300K+ for physicians) — volume of turnover events makes this the key financial lever
