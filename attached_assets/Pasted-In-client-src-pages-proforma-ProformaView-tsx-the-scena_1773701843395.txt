In client/src/pages/proforma/ProformaView.tsx, the scenario comparison
  feature exists but is not discoverable or usable. Fix the following issues:

  ---

  ISSUE 1 — Move Compare panel to top, below the hero KPIs

  Currently the comparison panel (data-testid="panel-scenario-comparison")
  renders near line 1694, well below the P&L table, sensitivity panel, and
  methodology accordion. Move it to appear immediately after the chart/view
  mode controls section (around line 420, after the scenario tabs row).
  Users who click Compare should see the result without scrolling.

  ---

  ISSUE 2 — Show "Compare" button always, not just when scenarios exist

  Currently the Compare button only renders when `scenarios.length > 0`:
    {scenarios.length > 0 && (
      <button onClick={() => setCompareMode(!compareMode)} ...>

  Change this: always show the Compare button. When `scenarios.length === 0`,
  clicking it should instead trigger `setShowSaveDialog(true)` with a prefilled
  name of "Scenario 1" and show an explanatory toast:
    toast({ title: "Save your first scenario", description: "Name this configuration, then change your deal settings to create a second one to compare." })

  This makes the button discoverable even on first visit.

  ---

  ISSUE 3 — Add a "How to use" hint under the scenario tabs row

  When `scenarios.length > 0` and `scenarios.length < MAX_SCENARIOS`, add
  a single line below the saved scenario pills:
    <p className="text-[11px] text-white/40 mt-1">
      Change your deal settings above, then save another scenario to compare.
    </p>

  This disappears once you hit MAX_SCENARIOS.

  ---

  ISSUE 4 — Fix the dashed line legend in the overlay chart

  At line ~1803, the legend items for saved scenarios use:
    style={{ borderTop: "2px dashed" }}
  This does not render a visible dashed line. Replace with an SVG line:
    <svg width="16" height="2" className="inline-block">
      <line x1="0" y1="1" x2="16" y2="1"
        stroke={SCENARIO_COLORS[idx % SCENARIO_COLORS.length]}
        strokeWidth="2"
        strokeDasharray={SCENARIO_DASHES[idx % SCENARIO_DASHES.length] || "none"}

  stroke="#1A1A1A").
